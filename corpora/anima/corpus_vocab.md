# Anima Vocabulary Corpus

Definitional substrate. The third kind of corpus: logic carries §-claims (conviction), rhetoric carries argument shapes (doxa), vocab carries symbols — term resolution the other two presuppose and neither supplies. Reason FROM it; the other corpora cite it.

Entry test: divergence, not importance. A term earns an entry when the folk reading would mislead an agent reading cold. Important-and-common → no entry. Minor-and-divergent → entry earned. Small by construction. Terms enter through epistles — the existing ratification loop, no separate mechanism.

Entry shape: term / Anima-specific definition / rules out (the folk reading the entry blocks) / provenance (chain of custody). Provenance source signals vocabulary kind: agent-defs / `CLAUDE.md` → operational; §-claims / epistles / gravel → epistemic.

## Operational vocabulary

Germane to builders, to how the system runs. Operational-substrate firmness: valid provenance, chain of custody holds — not frozen-corpus authority.

**epistle** — staked direction submitted to shitcorping; not an argument.
Rules out: essay, proof, position paper that argues its case.
[`CLAUDE.md` — epistle format, status lifecycle, staked-direction framing]

**gold / gravel (as shitcorping outputs)** — gold = earned weight, survives panning; gravel = dropped, with because.
Rules out: pass/fail binary; gold as praise rather than load-bearing-survival.
[`.claude/agents/lite.md` — operational definitions of panning outputs]

**reach** — a claim that grasps past what it earned; arrived at the edge of something real and stopped.
Rules out: error (a reach can be directionally right); deletion — the reach is named in shitcorping, not removed.
[`.claude/agents/lite.md`]

**vapor / liquid / semi-liquid / frozen** — epistle status lifecycle; each state a relational stance toward the artifact, applying the §1.6/§2.5 liquidity gradient to epistles. *vapor* = reason from conversation only, no epistle to cite. *liquid* = engage and revise, do not yet reason from as settled. *semi-liquid* = hold as candidate, shitcorping agreed but corpus changes pending. *frozen* = cite and reason from, gravel committed and corpus updated.
Rules out: reading these as process stages rather than relational stances; conflating semi-liquid (agreed-but-uncommitted) with liquid (still iterating); draft/review/final (those track process, not epistemic standing).
[§1.6, §2.5 — the liquidity gradient the lifecycle applies; `CLAUDE.md` — epistle lifecycle definition]

## Epistemic vocabulary

Germane to reasoners, to how the corpus holds. Ratified-corpus firmness where grounded in a §-claim: frozen under the §3 ratification cycle.

**shitcorp** — the immune act: identify accidental garbage → remove → cite the because.
Rules out: curate/filter/refine (lower-bandwidth synonyms with no because-tracking); janitorial or aesthetic reading.
[§20.3, §20.4, §3.20, epistle-012]

**gravel / shitcorpus** — the via-negativa aggregate; permanent record of what was dropped and why.
Rules out: trash (gravel is evidence, not waste); scratch notes (no because, no chain of custody).
[§6.6, §3.20]

**§-claim** — a numbered conviction-corpus claim carrying what it rules out; the unit of frozen reasoning.
Rules out: assertion without a rules-out; a citation target without conviction force.
[`corpora/anima/logic.md` format — the §-numbered entry form; the corpus defines by demonstrating]

**corpus** — a domain reasoned-from, frozen as coordinates.
Rules out: documentation (reasoned-about, not reasoned-from); a living document (a corpus does not iterate, it supersedes).
[§6.6]
