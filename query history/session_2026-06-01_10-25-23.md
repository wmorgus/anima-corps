## Query 1

### User Prompt

> "feature telos" -- what other category of telos exist

### Response Summary

Off the top — rough taxonomy:

- **Feature telos** — what this capability is for
- **User telos** — what the person is trying to accomplish (may not match feature)
- **Product telos** — what the whole artifact is for
- **Organizational telos** — why the org building it exists
- **Architectural telos** — what a structural decision is aimed at (often: ruling something out)
- **Process telos** — what a workflow or practice is for (e.g., code review, retros)
