Epistle 028 — Four Causes, Four Artifacts. Where final cause is buried.
Topic: Knowledge artifacts in a documentation corpus map onto the four causes. The gradient is diagnostic: it locates where final cause is absent, implicit, or buried. Extends Epistle 025's frame from fault types to artifact classes.

The move. Epistle 025 read the four causes (§13.6) as failures — final→spec fault, efficient→epistemic, material→substrate, formal→craft. 025 maps causes onto *what is broken*. This epistle maps causes onto *what is written down* — the artifact classes of an existing documentation corpus. Same four causes, different surface. 025 is a fault census; this is a documentation census. The new payload: run the census over a real doc corpus and read which cause has no artifact carrying it.

**The artifact gradient.**
- **Material cause → code, API docs, changelogs.** The stuff and its surface. What the system is made of, what it exposes, what changed in it.
- **Formal cause → ADRs, architecture decisions, schemas.** The intrinsic shape. How the parts are arranged and bounded.
- **Efficient cause → the executor (its prompt).** What brought the artifact about. The executor-as-agent is not a document. But the prompt that constitutes it is (§1.12, §6a.2) — efficient cause has an artifact home, the prompt; the actor running does not.
- **Final cause → telos statement, constraints, decisions log.** What the system is *for*. The end the structure serves.

Conventional software documentation covers material and formal richly — code, API docs, changelogs, ADRs are mature genres with tooling. Final cause is absent, implicit, or buried. The gradient is diagnostic because the gap is *systematic*, not incidental: the two lower causes have artifact homes; the top one usually does not. Point the census at a corpus and the hole is in the same place every time.

**Why now, at the executor level.** Implicit final-cause carriage worked while executors had heads. Developers held the telos in mind; the doc corpus could omit it because the person carrying it out already knew what it was for. AI executors break the carriage — no head to hold it in. §13.9 stakes the economy-level "why now": LLMs collapsed the cost of producing all registers. This is sharper at the executor level — LLMs did not only collapse the cost of writing the missing register; they removed the substrate that made omitting it survivable. The implicit channel is gone. The final-cause artifact stops being good hygiene and becomes load-bearing, because the only carrier that ever held it implicitly is no longer the one executing.

**The default failure mode.** Without final cause documented, the executor still optimizes — it cannot do nothing. It optimizes the causes that *are* written: formal (make the structure elegant) or material (make the code clean). Both feel like progress; neither is teleological. §13.6 stakes effort-without-telos is exertion, pattern-without-telos is shape, stuff-without-telos is matter. This names the specific substitution §13.6 left as a list: efficient cause, deprived of final, defaults *downward* into the two lower causes. The executor does not idle when telos is missing — it slides to the nearest documented cause and polishes. The output is exertion and shape, plating well. §1.11's process-without-purpose ruling-out, made concrete and predictive: this is *which* purposeless process you get, and why it looks like work.

**Constraints are final cause in negative form.** An executor with no constraints cannot tell adaptation from defection. Knowing what the system must *not* do is often more informative about the real end than knowing what it does. Constraints are not a fifth artifact class — they are final cause expressed apophatically. The corpus's own method: every §-claim carries a "Rules out"; telos.md is built from ruling-outs. The end is staked by its boundary. This gives Epistle 026's chef-deviation tension teeth: the line between adaptation and defection *is* the constraint set. A chef who sears when the recipe says braise has adapted if she stayed inside the rules-out, defected if she crossed it. The constraint set is where 026's open authority question gets a surface it can be measured against — even where 026 leaves *when* she may cross it unresolved.

**The decisions log: why-not over what.** Why things are the way they are distinguishes load-bearing structure from accident. Most projects over-document what they do and under-document what they considered and rejected. The decisions log — the why-nots — is the shitcorpus (§6.6) at project-documentation scale: §3.20's discard-as-act, run on project knowledge artifacts instead of agent reasoning. The shape of what a project *dropped* is evidence of what it is for, and §3.20 holds the same way here — the via negativa is invisible until written. An undocumented why-not is structurally indistinguishable from a decision never made. The decisions log is the project-scale `corpus_shitcorpus`.

**Produced-by and documents-about are orthogonal relations.** The seam with frozen Epistle 025 is staked, not waved off. 025 puts the *because* in efficient cause — "the because, what brought it about," the artifact's maker-story, causal-productive. This epistle puts the *because* of the decisions log in final cause — the log documents why this end and not that one, intentional-final. Not a contradiction. Two predications on one artifact, indexed differently. A decisions log was **produced by** an efficient cause (the act of deciding) AND **documents** final-cause reasoning (what end was served, what was ruled out). Made-by and documents-about are orthogonal: every artifact has both a maker-story and a content-it-carries, and these need not name the same cause. 025's *because* answers *what produced this?* — efficient. This epistle's *because* answers *what does this carry?* — final. The phrase "the because" bears both because the two relations index different objects on the same artifact. Because 025 is frozen, the seam is made visible and staked here, not left as a silent collision. [→ Epistle 025]

**Cut the actor from its prompt.** The flat "efficient cause is not an artifact" overstates — it would contradict §1.12/§6a.2 (prompt-as-artifact). The distinction: the **actor** (Hermes executing) is not a document; the **prompt** that constitutes it is, and it is first-class, ratifiable, citable. So efficient cause does have an artifact home — the prompt — even though the executor-as-agent is not itself a doc class. The maker-story has a record; the maker does not. [→ §1.12, §6a.2]

Decoration kept knowingly: the four-causes frame is not new ore — Epistle 025 froze it. What earns its keep here is the *gradient with the hole in a fixed place* and the executor-substrate mechanism (§13.9 sharpened). The diagnostic is the claim; the artifact list is the image that makes the hole locatable. [→ §1.11, §1.12, §3.20, §6.6, §6a.2, §6a.10, §13.6, §13.9, Epistle 025, Epistle 026]
