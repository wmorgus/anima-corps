Epistle 022 — Falsification by case: the evaluation_record and thick-case method
Topic: How to falsify whether Anima structurally improves agent reasoning when the load-bearing claims (F4, F5) have zero instrumentation and quantitative protocols are underpowered. Stake the missing artifact type, the case-documentation instrument, and what Stake's reframe requires the corpus to build.

The frame. Conversation built a falsification methodology, then a responsive Stake evaluation reframed it mid-development: the instrument for the architecture's central claims is not a metric, it is a documented case. This epistle holds both — the methodology and the reframe — and names the corpus gap the reframe opens. It stakes the conviction; it does not run the experiments or build the type (that is downstream, see Scope).

---

Gold 1 — `evaluation_record` artifact type + `evaluates` edge. The corpus has `conviction_stake` and `empirical_record` joined by `tests` (§4.9, §4.8). No type exists for a judgment that is *rendered* but not *measured*. Ground control (§11.7) renders exactly this — validates that actions go somewhere, distinguishes compliance from ratification, notices drift — and produces nothing ratifiable. The reading register has no output artifact. Stake the gap: `evaluation_record` is the missing type; `evaluates` is the edge joining it to the artifact judged. `evaluates` is semantically distinct from `tests`: `tests` interrogates a claim under measurement (different kind, evaluative-by-protocol, §4.8); `evaluates` interrogates a case under documented reading (different kind, evaluative-by-judgment). Both belong to the §4.8 typed-edge family; neither reduces to the other. Connects to §13.13(2): the programme-level diagnostic "requires reading the supersession record at scale" — that read needs the record to be readable, and a rendered judgment with no artifact type is not on the surface to be read. The `evaluation_record` is the diagnostic's missing input.

Gold 2 — Thick case documentation as legitimate falsification instrument. §14.1 names the falsifiable surface as thin: F1 alone instrumented, F2–F5 zero sensors. F4–F5 (§14.4) are load-bearing with no protocol. Stake: for claims where a quantitative protocol is underpowered or destroys what it measures (F4, F5 territory), a well-documented disconfirming case is a *harder* falsification instrument than an underpowered metric. A disconfirming case is harder to dismiss than a non-significant result on an underpowered test — the metric's null is consistent with "no effect" and "couldn't see it"; the documented failure is not. This is not retreat from falsifiability. It is naming the correct instrument for the claim type. Popper's surface (§14) admits more than one instrument; the metric is one, the case is another.

Gold 3 — Case accumulation as instrument for the §13.13 programme-level diagnostic. §13.13 stakes the Lakatosian progressive/degenerative tribunal and says plainly: "the architecture does not yet have a measurement protocol." Stake: the accumulated `evaluation_record` corpus *is* that instrument. "Is the architecture anticipating novel cases or absorbing known ones?" is answerable by reading the case record — diachronic, no quantitative protocol required. The question §13.13 leaves open (what cadence, what counts as registrable anticipation) is operationalized as: each documented case carries the date the architecture named the anomaly's shape and the date the world delivered it. Progressive = named-before-delivered, on the record. The case library is where that read stands.

Gold 4 — Case library as substrate the §15.17 reading-skill residual operates through — NOT its closure. §15.17 stakes the reading-skill residual as architecturally unaddressable: no structural substitute for apprenticeship; a reader arriving after the cycle cannot tell staked from laundered without independent reconstruction. The case library does not close this. It is the substrate the residual operates *through*: naturalistic generalization — a reader recognizing their situation in a documented case — is the transfer mechanism for reading skill, but the recognition still requires a reader who can tell a staked case from a laundered one. The corpus can be complete, traversable, and fully populated with `evaluation_record`s and still be misread. §15.17's stake holds intact: the library supplies cases to read; it does not supply the skill to read them. Distinction load-bearing — collapsing it would relaunder the closure §15.17 explicitly refuses.

---

The three experiments (practical output of the methodology — named, not over-built).

E1 — Adversarial-corpus case study. Feed a deliberately contradictory corpus. Document whether the architecture surfaces the contradiction or launders it. Disconfirming case at F4 / §13.13 level. Strongest disconfirming-case candidate: a documented laundering is harder to dismiss than an underpowered reversal-rate metric. Instruments F4's named shitcorpus sensor directly — §14.4 already names the `discard_record` surface (§3.20, §4.9) as the F4 sensor; the adversarial corpus drives candidates into that surface and the `evaluation_record` reads whether the contradiction landed there or vanished silently.

E2 — Longitudinal transfer as documented case. Task-1 → task-2 in sequence. Instrument: a written `evaluation_record` of whether task-2 reasoning recognized its situation in the frozen task-1 corpus (naturalistic generalization, Gold 4's mechanism). Not a quality-delta metric. Tests the accumulation claim (Gold 3) at the dyad scale before the corpus scale.

E3 — Mid-task perturbation as Lakatos/Stake hinge. Introduce a requirement change mid-execution. The moment of interest is the anomaly-classification act: belt or core? Document whether frozen principles were treated as belt (revisable, §13.13 protective belt) or core (defended, §13.1 hard core). The *difficulty* of that classification is the finding — §13.12 already stakes that anomalies do not arrive pre-labeled as falsifiers; E3 documents the interpretive labor of labeling one in real time.

Corpus connections named.

§11.7 — ground control is the register that renders the judgment `evaluation_record` records. The type gives the reading register an output artifact it currently lacks.
§13.13 — Lakatos tribunal ratified; measurement protocol open. This epistle proposes the instrument (Gold 3): case accumulation, not metric.
§14.1 / §14.4 — F4's shitcorpus sensor (`discard_record`, §3.20/§4.9) is instrumented by E1. F5 (supersession append-only vs. mutable, explicitly unstaked) is addressed by the experiments as documented cases rather than left as a named gap.
§15.17 — unaddressable; case library is substrate only (Gold 4). Not touched as closure.

The 020 seam. Epistle 020 stakes the severity register: via positiva, "what was tried against this §-claim and what survived" — attestation-shaped, the readable face of `tests` edges. `evaluation_record` is via the *rendered judgment*: ground control's reading of a case, the face of an `evaluates` edge. Adjacent, distinct. The severity register answers "what survived the attack on this claim"; the `evaluation_record` answers "what did the reader judge about this case." A severity-register row may *cite* an `evaluation_record` as the attempt-artifact when the attempt was a documented case rather than a protocol — but the two are different speech acts (attest-survival vs. render-judgment) at different surfaces. Name the seam; do not fuse them. [→ Epistle 020, §1.14, §4.8]
