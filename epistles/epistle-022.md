Epistle 022 — Telos as zeroth-class artifact
Topic: The telos is not the top of the first-class taxonomy. Different kind. Zeroth-class — prayer mode, not program mode.

Start with the picture the corpus already grants. Teleological software is a debate case: resolution at the top, contentions under it, sub-points under those — everything telescopes downward (logic §7, §5.5). §1.12 ranks the registers of that case by obligation type and ratification position. The telos sits at the head of every chain (§1.12 row: vision/telos artifact — "constrains everything downstream"). Natural move: call it first-class, highest-ranked. **That move is wrong.** The telos is not the top of the ladder. It is not on the ladder. Different speech act — different container.

**1. The speech act is different.** Programs and arguments carry completion conditions. A program executes or crashes. An argument is won or falsified. Both can be *finished* and both can be *defeated by the world*. A prayer cannot. A prayer commits a direction under uncertainty. Implementation failure does not falsify it — you can fail to build the thing and the reaching toward it stands untouched. Only one thing falsifies a prayer: changing what you reach toward. The telos doc is prayer. Every §-claim below it is a leg of the research experiment the telos is running — and an experiment can return null without unmaking the question it asked. The core does not move because the core is not the kind of thing the world votes on.

**2. Zeroth-class, defined by one inverted test.** First-class artifacts (§1.12): load-bearing, ratifiable, citable, not derivative of another register. The telos passes load-bearing, citable, non-derivative. It *fails ratifiable — on purpose.* It is not argued-for from inside the system. It is committed-to. Terminal node of the derivation graph: every other artifact answers "does this serve the telos?" The telos answers only "is this the right end?" — and that question has no seat inside the system. A node every arrow points at and none point out of is not the top of the taxonomy. It is the floor the taxonomy stands on. Zeroth.

**3. Not arbitrary — prior.** Reach: zeroth-class is not exemption from justification. Every prayer is made on a meta-bet — that it is worth praying at all, that agency might be enacted. That bet also lives outside the system; it does not get a §-number either. The telos is not unjustified. It is *pre*-justified — justified at a layer the corpus does not adjudicate because the corpus is downstream of it. §13.1 already says this from the other side: the conviction came first, the architecture is what the bet looks like committed-to literally. 022 names the container that conviction lives in. Tension held: "outside the system" and "still answerable" coexist. The meta-bet is answerable — but not here, not by these instruments. Flag: where it *is* answerable, 022 does not say.

**4. The wrongness of the motion is the signal.** Practical warrant for a separate doc, read straight off logic.md: it has too much motion. Some of what sits in there should never move again, forever — and it moves. That is the tell. Prayer and programme have different permanence profiles; forcing them into one container makes the prayer look revisable and the programme look sacred, both backwards. The discomfort of watching a never-should-move claim move is the corpus asking for a second container. (Belt vs. hard core, §13.13: the belt is *supposed* to move; the hard core is not. The telos doc is where the hard core lives undisturbed by belt motion. Decoration — but it places the new container against structure the corpus already ratified.)

**5. Four slots, or it is a label.** For the telos doc to be derivable-from — for "does this serve the telos?" to have a checkable answer — it must hold four things:
- **Resolution** — what changes in the world when this works. The debate-case top line.
- **Ruling-outs** — what the telos rejects. *The teeth.* A telos without exclusions is a label, not a commitment — the same standard every §-claim meets with its "rules out." A prayer that forbids nothing reaches toward nothing.
- **Value ordering** — when capabilities conflict, what wins and why. Without it the telos cannot adjudicate the conflicts its own derivations generate.
- **Derivation bridges** — explicit links to the first-class artifacts that follow. The arrows out, made visible, so the orphan-check in 023 is possible at all.

The telos doc is compositional, not monolithic. These slots need not share one permanence profile — resolution may be more fixed than value-ordering. That compositionality is not a footnote; 023 makes it load-bearing.

Net: the four-tier gradient (§2.1) is the permanence model for first-class artifacts. The telos is not in it — not frozen-est, not anything-est on that scale. It is off the scale, prior to it. §1.11/§1.12 grant the telos a row; 022 says the row is in the wrong table.

Open: the meta-bet's home (where the prayer-to-pray is adjudicated) is named and not located. Open: whether all four slots are mandatory or some telos docs run thinner. Cross-refs — §1.11, §1.12, §13.1, §2.1, logic §7, §5.5, §13.13. Depended-on-by: Epistle 023.
