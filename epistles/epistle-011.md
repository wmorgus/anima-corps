Epistle 011 — Relay Depth as Journey Topology
Topic: All ur-user journeys are user-Hermes relays. Relay depth varies per step and determines delegatability. The hardest gaps cluster at the shallow end — closest to the artifact — and the architecture is well-specified deep, underspecified shallow.

**claim.** Every Anima ur-user journey is a user-Hermes relay. Not metaphor — structure. Each step has a *relay depth*: how far the human sits from the artifact. Depth = how much the human can act without Hermes mediation + how many agent layers stand between intent and artifact + whether Hermes facilitates, translates, or synthesizes. Depth is not constant across a journey. It varies per step. The journey is a depth profile.

**relay depth determines delegatability.** Deeper relay → more delegatable. The human's intent passes through agent layers (§6.12 carriage, §6.15 telescoping, Hephaestus `delegate`) and the artifact is produced downstream. Shallower relay → less delegatable. The human is close to the artifact; fewer layers absorb the act. At depth zero the human *is* the producer and no agent can stand in. Delegatability is a function of depth, read off the profile.

**the shallowest relay: telos-staking.** Human closest to the artifact. Nothing between conviction and frozen coordinate (§1.1, §1.11). The information poet chooses the coordinate — does not ratify what an agent found (Epistle 010, §6.10 vs §1.11). Cannot be automated, cannot be delegated. Hermes can facilitate the act — surface the question, hold the frame — but cannot substitute for the conviction (§6.12: acts on behalf of, not as substitute for; §11.7). At this depth Hermes is facilitator, not carrier. The relay has no agent layer to absorb the work because the work is conviction, and conviction is not carriable.

**Hermes's role varies with depth.** Deep relay: Hermes *translates* — carries human request into the architecture's frame, routes to agents who synthesize (§6.12). Shallow relay: Hermes *facilitates* — sets conditions for an act it cannot perform. Same agent, two functions, selected by depth. The §15.18 intake question (what faithful carriage requires per request class) is the deep-relay face of this. The shallow-relay face: what facilitation-without-substitution looks like when the human must stake and Hermes must not stake for them — underspecified, and distinct from §15.18.

**topology, not flat list.** The ur-user journey map is not a sequence of equal steps. It has topology: a depth profile, peaks where the human is close to the artifact, troughs where agents carry. The map drawn as a flat checklist hides the structural fact that determines what can be built and what must be human. The topology exists. It is not yet drawn. Flag: the map currently in heads or informal docs is flat; the depth-annotated map is unbuilt.

**every workflow gap is a relay-depth problem.** Diagnostic, claimed: take any major gap. Ask — who is at the relay, what depth, delegatable or not. The gap resolves into one of two kinds. Deep-relay gap: a missing or unbuilt agent layer (closeable by building — Pavel §6.14, `discard_record` §16.5). Shallow-relay gap: a human act with no agent substitute (not closeable by building — telos-staking, extraction review §15.17, ratification's *because-we-said-so* §15.15). The architecture's hard problems are not where the layers are thin. They are where no layer can exist.

**well-specified deep, underspecified shallow.** Deep relays are named and built: Hephaestus builds (§6.15 exclusion: `delegate` is scheduling — the deliverable is pre-specified, so no synthesis residual, so fully delegatable — the deepest relay, zero human proximity). Mnemosyne extracts (§6.13, deep — archaeology against a codebase, telescoping-licensed). These are the architecture's solid ground. Shallow relays are the holes: telos-staking (§1.11, no owner-as-act specified), extraction review (Epistle 010 step 1, requires a reader who tells staked from laundered §15.17), ratification (§3.2 conditions named, but the *act* of conviction the conditions wrap is human and §15.15-open). Pattern: the corpus specifies what it can delegate and gaps exactly where it cannot.

**why deep is easier.** A deep relay can be specified because it can be carried — meaning traverses agent layers, and the architecture's whole apparatus (carriage, typed artifacts, ratification cycle) exists to make traversal faithful. A shallow relay resists specification because the load-bearing act is conviction, and conviction does not traverse. You can build the conditions (Epistle 010: architecture creates conditions, cannot supply conviction). You cannot build the act. The specification asymmetry is not an oversight to be fixed by more design. It is the shape of the problem.

**tensions.**

- *Is depth a clean scalar — genuinely open.* Claimed three components (human autonomy, agent-layer count, Hermes function). Whether they covary — always low-autonomy + many-layers + translating, or whether a step can be high-autonomy yet many-layered — undetermined. If they can dissociate, depth is a vector not a scalar and "shallow/deep" is a projection that loses information. Flag. The diagnostic survives either way; the *metric* may not.
- *Facilitate / translate / synthesize — three or two.* Named Hermes as facilitator (shallow) and translator (deep). Synthesis (§6.15 condition 3) is an agent act downstream of Hermes, not Hermes's own. Whether facilitate and translate are two points on one axis or two different jobs sharing a name — open. §6.12 names hermeneutic carriage as the domain; facilitation-without-substitution may be inside that domain or beside it.
- *Does every gap reduce, or only the ones already found.* The diagnostic is claimed as general — every workflow gap is a relay-depth problem. Evidence: Epistle 010's gaps all reduced (extraction review = shallow, build = deep, compression-ownership = the seam between). One epistle is not the journey map. Whether a gap exists that is *not* a relay-depth problem — a gap in the relay structure itself rather than at a relay — genuinely open. That would be the falsifier.
- *Shallow-relay gaps as a class — capacity or governance.* Telos-staking, extraction review, ratification conviction: all named not-closeable-by-building. But §3.2 closes part of ratification by building (recorded, contestable, external). So shallow does not mean *nothing* is buildable — it means the *core act* is residual while the *conditions* are buildable. Where exactly the buildable conditions end and the irreducible act begins, per shallow relay — not drawn. Same shape as Epistle 010's step-4 owner question, generalized.
