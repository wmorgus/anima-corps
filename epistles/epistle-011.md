Epistle 011 — The Relay
Topic: Every ur-user journey is a relay — stated intent → Hermes cooks as deep as needed → result returned. Depth is a property of the cook, not the human's position. Delegatability reads off cook-depth. The output is generative: it unlocks intents the human couldn't state before. Every workflow gap is a relay-depth problem.

**claim.** Every Anima ur-user journey is a relay. Not metaphor — structure. The unit: human states intent → Hermes cooks as deep as needed → result returned. One unit, universal. The journey is not a sequence of distinct steps with distinct shapes. It is this single unit, run again and again. Extends §6.12: hermeneutic carriage is not just the front-door act, it is the per-interaction unit the whole journey is built from.

**depth is a property of the cook, not the human.** The human is fixed. They sit at the intent level and do not move — across the whole journey, the human's position is constant: state intent, receive result. What varies is the cook. Hermes carries the intent downstream and the cook runs as deep as the intent requires: shallow (Hermes facilitates, no agent layer absorbs the act) to deep (request traverses agent layers, artifact produced far downstream — §6.12 carriage, §6.15 telescoping, Hephaestus `delegate`). Depth is inside the cook. The old frame had the human moving toward and away from the artifact. The human never moves. Hermes varies in depth.

**delegatability reads off cook-depth.** One axis. Deep cook → agent layers absorb the act → delegatable. The deepest: `delegate` is scheduling, deliverable pre-specified, no synthesis residual, fully delegatable (§6.15 exclusion). Shallow cook → fewer layers absorb → less delegatable. Cook-depth zero → the residual act is irreducibly human and no agent stands in. This unifies under one reading: the §6.15 telescoping license (when an act decomposes into agent-carried sub-scopes), the §6.15 Hephaestus exclusion (when it is pre-specified scheduling, not synthesis), and §1.11 non-delegatability (when the act is conviction). All three are points on the cook-depth axis.

**cook-depth zero: telos-staking is the floor.** Human stakes the coordinate. Nothing between conviction and frozen coordinate (§1.1, §1.11). The information poet chooses — does not ratify what an agent found (Epistle 010). Cannot be automated, cannot be delegated. Hermes facilitates — surfaces the question, holds the frame — but does not substitute for the conviction (§6.12: on behalf of, not as substitute for; §11.7). At cook-depth zero there is no agent layer to absorb the work because the work is conviction, and conviction does not traverse (Epistle 010: architecture creates conditions, cannot supply conviction). This is the floor of the depth axis, not a separate kind of thing.

**the relay output is generative.** A result returned does not close the journey. It unlocks intents the human could not state before — the human now sees the next question because the last result landed. The journey is recursively chained relay cycles: intent → cook → result → new intent → cook → result. The chain extends each time a result lands. This is the dynamic structure of journeys, and it is what the journey *is* — not a fixed list of steps, a generative loop that grows.

**pre-cook clarification is a converging graph; the cook is sequential.** Before the cook runs, intent must reach a clean stated state. That phase is a directed graph with cycles allowed: human gathers context, Hermes clarifies, back-and-forth, fan-out (§6.11 — intake checks shaped to request type, not a uniform ritual). The phase converges to a clean stated-intent state. Then the cook runs, and the cook is sequential under §6.12 — dialogic carriage, not spatially decomposable, Hermes not licensed for telescoping. No tension: cycles and fan-out live in the clarification phase only; once the cook begins it is sequential. Two regimes, cleanly separated by the stated-intent boundary.

**new intents are feedback, not gaps.** When a relay output surfaces an intent the system has no charted relay for, that is an uncharted case — feedback signal to the anima system authors, folded back via the evolution loop (§13.10, §3.5). The human gathering a new intent is not exceeding their standing; surfacing intents the corpus has not charted is the expected mechanism of a generative system (§15.15: the "we" stakes what it charts; new intents are how the charted set grows). Not a hole to patch. The generativity *is* the mechanism. Uncharted relay → evolution loop → charted relay.

**every workflow gap is a relay-depth problem.** Diagnostic, claimed: take any major gap. Ask — what cook-depth does this relay require, and can it be carried. The gap resolves into one of two kinds. Deep-relay gap: a missing or unbuilt agent layer — closeable by building (Pavel §6.14, `discard_record` §16.5). Shallow-relay gap: a human act with no agent substitute — not closeable by building (telos-staking, extraction review §15.17, ratification's *because-we-said-so* §15.15). The architecture's hard problems are not where the layers are thin. They are where no layer can exist because the cook cannot go deeper without something carrying conviction, and nothing carries conviction.

**the specification asymmetry is structural.** Deep relays are well-specified and built; shallow relays are underspecified. Not an oversight. A relay can be specified iff it can be carried — meaning traverses agent layers, and the architecture's whole apparatus (carriage, typed artifacts, ratification cycle) exists to make traversal faithful (extends §13.6: the apparatus exists to carry the *because* toward the telos). A shallow relay resists specification because the load-bearing act is conviction, and conviction does not carry. You can build the conditions; you cannot build the act. The corpus specifies what it can delegate and gaps exactly where it cannot. The asymmetry is the shape of the problem, not a stage of incompleteness — it reframes the corpus's open shallow-end gaps (§15.15, §15.17) as necessary, not unfinished.

**tensions.**

- *Is cook-depth a clean scalar — genuinely open.* Candidate components: human autonomy at the residual act, agent-layer count, Hermes function (facilitate vs. translate). Whether they covary — always low-autonomy + few-layers + facilitating — or can dissociate, undetermined. If they dissociate, depth is a vector not a scalar and "shallow/deep" is a projection that loses information. Flag. The diagnostic survives either way; the *metric* may not.
