Epistle 013 — The Vocabulary Corpus
Topic: Anima needs a declared vocabulary corpus — a dedicated file that is the definitional substrate the other corpora build on. Not a dictionary. Only terms Anima uses with load-bearing specificity: where the folk reading would mislead an agent reading cold. Population pass zero — bare essentials. Stakes the criterion, the ratification standard, the first population, and provenance as chain of custody.

**claim.** Vocabulary is a third kind of corpus. Logic has §-claims (conviction). Rhetoric has argument shapes (doxa). Vocabulary is definitional substrate — term resolution the other two presuppose and neither supplies. A dedicated file: `corpus_vocab.md`. Pass zero populates the terms already load-bearing in the corpora. The list is open; the ratification standard governs growth.

**why a third kind.** §20.2 stakes vocabulary compression as read-time efficiency — a precise term delivers act + object + orientation in one token. The compression only cashes out if the agent resolves the term to the Anima-specific meaning, not the folk one. Logic §-claims and rhetoric moves *use* terms like "shitcorp," "gravel," "epistle"; neither *defines* them. An agent reasoning from logic must already know what "shitcorp" rules out, or the §-claim is read wrong. The vocab corpus is what makes §20.2's compression payable: the table the reader resolves against. The logic corpus already names a domain-model register (the ratified vocabulary the other registers cite; logic line 58) — but names it as a row, self-referentially folded into logic itself. This epistle stakes it as a separate artifact. Flag: tension with that self-reference. Logic says "this corpus is Anima's domain model register." Either the vocab corpus extracts that function into its own file (and logic cites it), or the two overlap and drift. Lean: extract. A register folded into the conviction corpus cannot be cited cleanly by rhetoric or ur-software without dragging logic's frame. Genuinely open which way it resolves.

**the ratification standard.** Not every term earns an entry. Test: does Anima use this term with enough specificity that the folk/common reading would mislead an agent reading cold? Load-bearing divergence from common usage, or obligation structure the agent needs to know cold (§1.5 — borrowed vocabulary carries obligation structure; the entry is where that obligation gets declared instead of silently imported). If the common reading is close enough, no entry — the term resolves correctly without help, and an entry would be inflation. The bar is divergence, not importance. A term can be important and common (no entry); a term can be minor and divergent (entry earned). This cuts against dictionary instinct: the vocab corpus is small by construction.

**entry shape.** Each entry carries four things: (1) the term; (2) the compressed Anima-specific definition; (3) what it rules out — the folk reading or near-synonym the entry exists to block; (4) provenance — the §-claim, epistle, or gravel record that grounded the meaning. Same shape as a logic §-claim minus the conviction force: a definition is not a stake about the world, it is a coordinate for reading stakes about the world.

**pass zero — bare essentials.** First population = terms already in use and already load-bearing:
- *shitcorp* — the immune act: identify accidental garbage → remove → cite the because. Rules out: curate/filter/refine (lower-bandwidth synonyms); janitorial or aesthetic reading. [§20.3, §20.4, §3.20, epistle-012]
- *epistle* — staked direction submitted to shitcorping; not an argument. Rules out: essay, proof, position paper that argues its case.
- *gravel / shitcorpus* — the via-negativa aggregate; permanent record of what was dropped and why. Rules out: trash (gravel is evidence); scratch notes. [§6.6, §3.20]
- *gold / gravel (as shitcorping outputs)* — gold = earned weight; gravel = dropped, with because. Rules out: pass/fail; gold as praise rather than load-bearing-survival.
- *vapor / liquid / frozen* — epistle status lifecycle: written-not-shitcorped / shitcorped-candidate / ratified-into-corpus. Rules out: draft/review/final (these track process, not epistemic standing). [§1.6, §2.5]
- *§-claim* — a numbered conviction-corpus claim carrying what it rules out. Rules out: assertion without a rules-out; a citation target without conviction force.
- *reach* — a claim that grasps past what it earned. Rules out: error (a reach can be directionally right); the reach is named in shitcorping, not deleted.
- *corpus* — a domain reasoned-*from*, frozen as coordinates. Rules out: documentation; a domain reasoned-*about*. [§6.6]

List not closed. The standard, not this enumeration, decides additions.

**provenance is chain of custody, not decoration.** Each entry traces to its source. A term whose meaning floats free of a §-claim or epistle is a definition without a because — structurally the §3.20 failure mode (the uncited discard) applied to definitions: a coordinate that cannot be checked against what grounded it. Provenance is what makes the vocab corpus trustworthy to reason *from*. Without it, the corpus is just assertion about what words mean — and an agent reading from it would launder that assertion as authority (the §3.15 staleness pattern, applied to vocabulary). The provenance link is also the colonization tripwire (§15.12, §15.16): when a vocab entry borrows a term from another corpus (logic ← ur-software, e.g.), the provenance trail makes the crossing visible. A term arriving without provenance is colonization laundered as elaboration.

**tensions.**

- T1 — Domain-model self-reference (logic line 58) vs. extracted vocab file. Logic claims the domain-model register as its own. This epistle stakes a separate artifact. Lean extract; not resolved. Whoever resolves it must say what logic cites and what the vocab file owns. Open.
- T2 — Entry-shape vs. §-claim drift. A vocab entry carries "rules out," same as a §-claim. Risk: an entry smuggles conviction force a definition shouldn't carry — the term's meaning gets argued instead of declared. Where does definition end and stake begin? Genuinely open. The accidental/essential cut (§20.3) may be the discriminator — a definition is accidental-removable from the claim it grounds; the claim's conviction is essential. Untested.
- T3 — Pass-zero closure. The list is staked as open, but every "open list" risks becoming a dumping ground (dictionary instinct, the thing the standard exists to resist). No mechanism named for *who* applies the ratification standard to a candidate term, or where that ratification is recorded. Don't know yet. Candidate: same shitcorping loop that sorts epistles, applied to term candidates.
