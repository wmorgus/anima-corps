Epistle 046 — The Authoring Layer Was Not Carried
Topic: The aZero build carried the six constellation agents and their tools but missed the corpus authoring layer — builder and lite. Corpus work has been running by hand, outside the system, the whole build.

---

**What was missed.**

`anima-corps/.claude/agents/builder.md` and `anima-corps/.claude/agents/lite.md` are the agents that author the corpus: builder writes epistles and commits gravel records, lite runs the shitcorping loop. The aZero build carried the constellation — Hermes, Clio, Heph, Urania, Mnemosyne, Pavel — and their tool surfaces. It did not carry these two. The authoring layer was treated as outside the system rather than as part of it.

**Why it matters.**

Corpus authoring is system work, and it has been running outside the system. Sessions 5–7 of this build wrote epistles, shitcorped them, and revised toward freeze — all by hand, in Claude Code, with no relay record, no substrate read, no provenance trail. That bypass is the exemption §3.3a names: "a master who exempts their own outputs from the quality check (shitcorping sweep) has the name and not the obligation — same asymmetric externality, one register over." §6.12a carries it as a rule — "the master shitcorps himself first." Authoring that skips the shitcorping loop, the relay record, and the Calliope provenance trail is the cultivation face exempting its own outputs. It ran exempt because the agents that perform the work were never seeded — the §4 substrate (the append-only store, no side channels) and the §3.3 externality requirement (the validator external to the validated) are the conditions the by-hand work bypassed, not the warrant for why the bypass is a fault. This epistle is the last piece of corpus work processed by hand. After this, authoring runs through the dojo.

**The fix.**

builder and lite become `prompt-*` artifacts in Calliope — `prompt-builder-001`, `prompt-lite-001` — invocable from the dojo face. This is not a new agent class. Per Epistle 045, the dojo face (Ari/Hermes) dispatches work at a configured intake depth; corpus authoring is one more kind of work it dispatches.

What builder and lite *are* relative to the constellation is open. By analogy to 045, they should live as `prompt-*` artifacts the face invokes — but 045 settled the face/agent distinction for *intake* faces (Ari/Hermes carrying a human request), and authoring is a different function. Whether builder and lite are faces, tool surface, or something else entirely requires re-running §6.1's harm-identity test — same harm, different material → face — against authoring work specifically. This epistle does not run that test. The classification is the open 045 left for the authoring agents, not a placement this record settles.

How the face loads the persona at invocation is also open. The §6a.10 instinct is a pull from the substrate rather than a caller push — 045 staked that instinct and said plainly the corpus has not ratified it. 046 inherits the open; it does not close it.

**Open.**

- *What the prompt artifacts carry.* builder and lite are written as Claude Code subagent definitions — persona, format rules, the two-act loop. What translates into a `prompt-*` artifact, what the dojo face needs to dispatch to them (invocation surface), and how that dispatch wires to the relay (dispatch wiring) is not yet specified. The shape of the configuration determines whether `prompt-*` holds it or a richer type is needed — the same open Epistle 045 left for faces, now inherited by the authoring agents.
