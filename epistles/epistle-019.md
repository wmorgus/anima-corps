Epistle 019 — One file, one question
Topic: logic.md answers two questions for two readers. That is the split condition. Stake the cut — who (agents.md) vs. how-must-it-work (logic.md) — and place the glossary on the same axis.

The principle. Every file answers one question. When a file answers two questions for different readers for different reasons, it splits. Not a style preference — a structural test. The driver is the question, not the topic.

What logic.md currently answers — two questions. (a) "What does Anima commit to?" — conviction. A stake plus what it rules out, falsified by the world. (b) "Who occupies which slot?" — specification. A role plus its instrument and harm-bearer, violated by a misfit occupant. §6.13 (Mnemosyne), §6.14 (Pavel), §6.17 (Daedalus) are slot specifications wearing conviction format: domain owned, reasoning obligation, harm-when-failed, rules-out-as-slot. They reason-from nothing. They name an occupant. Two questions, two speech acts, two failure modes (the world vs. the roster), two readers, two rates of change — one file. That is the split condition met. (The kind-distinction this rides on is already on record: §6a.2 names agent identity as the `prompt-as-artifact` register, distinct from conviction. Not re-staked here. The load-bearing claim is the single-question test, not the kind.)

The cut — who vs. how-must-it-work. The boundary between the new file and logic.md falls on the question, not on roster-vs-conviction.

`agents.md` answers "who is this agent?" — name (§6.8), domain owned (§6.5, the concept not the function), instrument, harm-when-failed + harm-bearer (§6.5 closing test), rules-out as a slot. Definitional artifact. Same register as the glossary: it names, it does not rule things out about the world.

`logic.md` keeps "how must this operation work for correctness?" — architectural commitments, not slot definitions:
- The Calliope query guard (§6.17 pre-verdict pull). Without it telos-awareness collapses into telos-origination. That is conviction — a claim about what the operation requires to stay correct.
- The telescoping license (§6.15). What the architecture permits a class of agents to do under three named conditions. Conviction about what the topology licenses, not a description of who anyone is.
- Agent context architecture (§6a). Tiers, delivery primitives, the four contradictions. Conviction.

This resolves the soft edge the earlier framing left open. Telescoping is a "how must it work," not a "who." Stays in logic.md. The operational guards stay. The cut is clean because it is the question that sorts, not the topic: §6.15 is *about* agents but *answers* how-must-it-work, so it stays. §6.13 is *about* an operation Mnemosyne performs but *answers* who-is-this-slot, so it moves.

The glossary on the same axis. The glossary answers a third question: "what does this mean?" — definitional, not conviction, not roster. Three files, three questions: what-does-it-mean (glossary), who-is-this-agent (agents.md), how-must-it-work (logic.md). The glossary and agents.md share a register — both name, neither rules out about the world. They are still two files because they answer two questions for two readers.

Named gaps.
- Glossary gap. The glossary register was ratified (§1.12 names it) but the file was never written. Named here. Same class as the Urania gap.
- Urania gap. Urania is referenced as domain owner (§6.5), telescoping-licensed (§6.15), review-gate occupant reading correctness/lineage (§8.19) — never a structured slot entry the way Mnemosyne/Pavel/Daedalus get. agents.md makes the hole loud: an empty row is louder than a name that happens not to recur. Same for any half-specified occupant (Hephaestus, Clio at §8.20 — asserted, not specified).

The schema is candidate, not ratified. Field set above lifted from what §6.13/§6.17 already carry. Don't know the exact set yet. Flag for the build.

Scope. This epistle stakes the shape (one file, one question) and the cut (who vs. how-must-it-work). It does not perform the migration. Moving slot entries out of logic.md, writing agents.md, writing the glossary file — later work. The epistle is the architectural stake, not the implementation. What is not yet decided: whether migrated §6.13/§6.14/§6.17 leave a stub pointer in logic.md or move whole; the ratified field set; the cross-file citation discipline once §-numbers span three files.
