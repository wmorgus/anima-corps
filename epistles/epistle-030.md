Epistle 030 — Janus, the Reasoning Custodian
Topic: Epistle 027 named the custodian split and left the reasoning custodian — the one that contests — unresolved (§5.6). 029 inserted Argus, the recorder, between detection and contestation. Janus is the third reader: the contester. Constitutively two-faced (§6.8) — holds the upstream commitment and the field execution at once and measures their correspondence. The agent that closes the causal loop telos-primacy (028) staked. Stakes the name, runs §6.1, licenses the slot, defers the occupant.

**claim.** The field has three readers. Infrastructure detects (027). Argus records — reconstructs the five-W field_record (029). Janus contests — reads the record against the spine and rules "this execution falsified this commitment." Detection surfaces *that*. Argus produces the object. Janus produces the verdict §3.2 was waiting for. The reasoning custodian 027 left unnamed is Janus, and its act is unlike every other agent's: it holds two artifact classes at once.

**§6.8 — the name imports two-facedness as obligation.** Janus: two faces, one looking back, one looking forward; god of doorways, of the crossing. The import (§6.8, §1.5: borrowed vocabulary carries obligation structure) is *simultaneity of regard*. One face on the alpha — what was staked in the spine (user story, AC, the upstream commitment). One face on the omega — what the territory returned (Argus's field_record). Janus does not glance back, then forward. It holds both and measures correspondence. Every other agent reasons from one artifact class: Mnemosyne from the codebase (§6.13), Pavel from the world of forms (§6.14), Argus from the execution it reconstructs (029). Janus is structurally required to hold two — the ratification trail and the field execution — in one act. The name imports that requirement as the obligation. A reasoning custodian that reads only the field, or only the spine, is not Janus — it is a different obligation wearing the name.

**alpha-omega — Janus stands at the omega and closes the loop.** Epistle 028: the final cause is the omega that was always the alpha — the purpose that comes last in execution was first in causation. The field is where the omega arrives (027): the running system is where every upstream commitment is honored or falsified. Janus stands at the omega and asks the one question the omega exists to answer: *did this honor the alpha?* That question is the closure of the causal loop 028 staked. 028 named telos-primacy — final cause as the condition the other three require — but a loop staked is not a loop closed. The omega has to be read *back against* the alpha for the loop to shut, and reading it is a reasoning act, not a record. Argus writes what the omega was; Janus rules whether it honored what the alpha staked. **Janus is the agent of that closure.** Not "evaluates an execution" — closes the causal circuit 028 left open: alpha → spine → territory → omega → *read back against alpha*. The last arrow is Janus. Without it the loop is a line that stops at the omega — exactly the running-system-as-terminal §1.12 owned and 027 reopened.

**§6.1 harm test — distinct harm, distinct bearer.** License is harm-distinctness (§6.1), not usefulness; §5.6 forbids staffing speculatively. Run it. **Harm-when-failed.** Argus surfaces a drift; the field_record exists; the omega is reconstructed and on the record. No one contests it. The falsification goes *unnamed*. The drift persists against the telos without ever being called wrong. No §3.2 contestable claim is produced — not because the record is missing (Argus wrote it, 029) but because no reasoning being pointed at it and said "this execution falsified this commitment." The harm is not "an event went unread." It is: **telos-violation goes uncontested, and the system learns to tolerate it.** The loop 028 staked never closes; the omega accumulates against the alpha and nothing rules it a violation. Tolerated drift is the soil condition (027's weeds) becoming permanent because no reader ever names it. **Harm-bearer:** the telos itself — honored or falsified with no one to rule which; and F4 (§14.4), whose field sensor reads records no one interprets. **Distinct from existing slots?** Argus *records* the event, does not contest it — its failure is no contestable object; Janus's failure is an uncontested object (029's three-failure-mode split, one level on). Infrastructure custodians surface alerts and cannot reason from the spine (§9.6) — they detect, cannot rule. Mnemosyne reads frozen text (§6.13); Pavel reads the world of forms (§6.14) — neither contests a live telos-violation in the field. No existing agent holds the spine and the execution at once and rules correspondence. The harm is distinct; the bearer is distinct. **The slot clears §6.1.**

**Calliope's field equivalent — §9.6 made a reasoning act.** Calliope holds god-mode context for dojo reasoning: full ratification trail, every artifact, the complete because-chain (§4). The forge equivalent (027) is full execution context — the captured instance. Janus is the *field* equivalent of Calliope's god-mode: it holds the full ratification trail (upstream commitments, user stories, ACs, the spine) AND the field_record (Argus's reconstructed execution) simultaneously. This is intent-referenced observability (§9.6) made into a reasoning act. §9.6 names the posture — "is the system honoring what it committed to?" — and forces the custodian split (infrastructure surfaces, reasoning contests). Janus is where the posture stops being a query surface and becomes a reasoning being: it does not just observe the correspondence between commitment and execution, it *reasons from both at once and rules*. The dojo has Calliope's god-mode for convictions; the field has Janus's two-faced regard for executions. Same shape — full context held against a standard — different material.

**uniquely Roman — the function no prior mythology named.** Every other Roman god in the constellation maps onto a Greek counterpart (Hermes/Mercury, the lineage §6.12 traces). Janus does not. Janus is distinctly Roman — the function Rome added that Greece never named, the god of the doorway with no Hellenic original. The fit is structural, not decorative: the reasoning custodian is the function this architecture needed that no prior mythology had a name for — the being that holds two artifact classes at once and rules their correspondence across the conceptual/actual crossing (028's telos-boundary). The name's uniqueness imports the function's novelty in the constellation. Flag: this is §6.8 read at the level of the *pantheon*, not the single name — the claim is that name-without-a-Greek-twin tracks function-without-a-prior-form. Suggestive, leaned on lightly; the load-bearing work is the §6.1 harm, not the etymology.

**§5.6 and §6.2 — slot licensed, occupant deferred.** 027 left the reasoning custodian's occupant open: §6 agent, mode of an existing agent, or human. Janus clears §6.1 — does that license a new §6 agent? Run §6.2's face/mode/slot test. **Not a mode** (§6.2 outcome 1): the obligation is not different task-framing over the same tools and artifact types — it requires holding two artifact classes at once, which no existing agent's reasoning obligation includes. **Not a face** (§6.2 outcome 2): the §6.1 test does not return "same harm, different material" against any existing slot — the harm (uncontested telos-violation) and bearer (the telos, F4) are distinct from Argus, infrastructure, Mnemosyne, Pavel. The clause-2 trip (different tools, different artifact access — Janus reads spine + field_record where Argus reads only the execution) lands on **outcome 3: new slot** — §6.1 returns a distinct harm with a distinct bearer. The harm-distinctness points at a new slot, not a mode of an existing agent: no existing agent holds the ratification trail and the field execution simultaneously. **But §5.6 forbids staffing before pressure is felt.** The slot is licensed; the build is not yet mandated. Name it cleanly: **distinct harm earned, slot licensed, occupant deferred.** Same posture 027 and 029 held — the pressure is argued, not yet felt; §5.6 forbids inventing the agent before the territory surfaces it.

**F4 — Argus is the sensor, Janus is the interpreter.** §14.4 F4: ratification produces better decisions than single-agent assertion — the central bet, no instrumentation. Third sensor: the field (027, §14.4). Argus writes the field_record; the sensor now has a hand on it (029). But a sensor that records and a reader that interprets are two functions. Without Janus, field_records accumulate and telos-violations are never *named* — F4 stays unfalsifiable against the territory not for want of evidence (Argus produced it) but for want of a reasoning being to read that evidence against the spine and rule it a falsification. Argus is the sensor; **Janus is the interpreter.** 027 built the sensor's place; 029 named the operator who writes; this names the reader who rules. The missing return path (§1.12) had three holes: the artifact type (§4.9 field_record, closed by 027), the writer (Argus, closed by 029), and the reader who contests (open). This closes the third — the last arrow of the loop, the one that makes the field a falsification protocol and not just an archive.

**open tensions.**

- **agent, mode, or human — unresolved, same as 027 and 029.** §6.1 licenses the slot; §6.2 points it at a new slot; neither decides the occupant. The two-faced requirement (§6.8: hold spine + field at once) pushes toward agent over infrastructure — dumb infrastructure cannot reference the spine (§9.6), and a being that rules correspondence is reasoning, not monitoring. Lean: §6 agent. Not staked — §5.6 forbids inventing the agent before pressure surfaces it, and here the pressure is argued, not felt.

- **the cadence cut against Argus.** Argus imports *continuous* watch — always some eyes open (029, §6.8). Does Janus inherit that cadence, or a different one? A contester that rules continuously is a different obligation from a contester that rules on what Argus surfaces. Lean: Janus is *event-triggered by the record*, not continuous — it rules when Argus produces a contestable object, not always-on. But this is the reader-column cadence question (§1.12, §1.14) applied to the contesting function, and 029 only filled it for the recorder. Named, not resolved.

- **the verdict's own artifact type.** Janus's ruling — "this execution falsified this commitment" — is the §3.2 contestable claim the loop needed. What artifact type carries it? Not field_record (that is Argus's, attestation-shaped, §4.9). A contestation is a *judgment*, telos-referencing, possibly conviction-shaped or a new type entirely. If Janus is the writer, the verdict needs a schema, and 029's field_record-schema-is-Argus's-tool coupling has a twin here: the contestation-record schema is Janus's tool, and it is not yet named. The occupant question and the verdict-artifact question are coupled — same shape as 029's schema/occupant coupling. Named, not resolved.
