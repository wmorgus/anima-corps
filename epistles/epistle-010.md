Epistle 010 — The Mnemo Extraction Gap
Topic: The sequence from "extracted" to "Anima-ready." Corpus names the pieces. Nobody assembles them into a playbook. The deepest hole is a telos-staking act the architecture cannot supply.

**claim.** Mnemosyne extraction is not a domain corpus. It is §6.10 output — thesis archaeology, liquid, what-the-codebase-argues. Between that and a frozen reasoning surface (§6.6) sits a five-step sequence nobody owns. Corpus names every piece. No §-claim assembles them into a succession with owners and review gates.

**what extraction hands you.** A picture of what *is*. Where the argument fractured without being named (§6.10). Not what to reason *from*. Liquid, not frozen tier. Mnemosyne's slot ends at the artifact (§6.13); the slot after it is unspecified.

**compression has no owner.** Extraction fragments → ratified domain corpus (§6.6, the `corpus_[codebase]_*.md` equivalent; register per §1.12). Someone must compress and ratify. Architecture does not name what or who. Worse — does not name who validates the compression was *honest* not merely schema-complete. This is §3.16 landing exactly here: a hollow corpus satisfying the form is indistinguishable from a genuine one without independent reconstruction. Capacity residual, not governance-addressable. The heaviest-load artifact class (prose, deliberative) born at the least-watched moment.

**Pavel's absence is load-bearing.** You have what the codebase *argues* (Mnemosyne, §6.13). Not where it *sits* (§6.14, §8.4). Positional diagnosis — inherited substrate debts, the world-of-forms coordinate, what's already paid and what's still owed — is [OPEN, unbuilt, blocking]. Build without it = build on undiagnosed substrate debt. The playbook has a structural hole exactly where §6.14 would step in. Two slots, two harms (§6.14): Mnemosyne's failure distorts the argument, Pavel's distorts the placement. The gap inherits both.

**telos not yet staked.** Extraction surfaces the argument the codebase *already makes*. Not the telos the build is oriented *toward* (§1.1, §1.11). Telos-staking is a separate act — human anchor stakes the end state, maps existing codebase against it: kept, superseded, acknowledged-as-debt. Until the telos artifact is frozen, downstream agents reason from pure archaeology. Extraction = where you came from. Someone must stake where you're going before the build cycle starts.

**intent spine, code end only.** Mnemosyne gives the code end of the spine. Upstream registers — user stories, acceptance criteria — don't exist, live informally in heads, or never ratified (§2.22, epistemic janitoring). Building from extraction without a ratified upstream spine = agents reason from what the code argues, not from committed human intent. Archaeology-without-a-record, reversed.

**the five-step shape — each currently unspecified as playbook.**

1. *Extraction review and triage.* Human anchor reads Mnemosyne output. Load-bearing fracture vs. ephemeral artifact of old context. Requires a reader who tells staked from laundered (§15.17) — not solvable architecturally.
2. *Positional diagnosis.* Pavel (§6.14). Unbuilt. Blocking.
3. *Telos-staking.* Human anchor, Hermes facilitating (§1.1, §1.11). Stakes intended end state; maps codebase against it; explicit on kept / superseded / debt.
4. *Domain corpus construction and ratification.* Compress extraction + telos stake → frozen corpus (§6.6). The ratification here must meet §3.2 — recorded, contestable, structurally external. No specified owner or review gate exists.
5. *Intent spine gap audit.* Before Hephaestus builds, name which upstream registers exist ratified and which are known gaps. Build proceeds with explicit acknowledgment, not silent absence.

**ground control's seam.** §11.7 distinguishes "the extraction ran" from "the extraction worked" — structural compliance vs. substantive ratification. The role exists. The succession and review procedure for *this specific transition* is specified nowhere.

**the deepest problem.** "Extracted" → "Anima-ready" requires a genuine telos-staking act that cannot be automated or delegated to an agent. The information poet chooses the coordinate (§1.11) — does not merely ratify what Mnemosyne found. The architecture creates the conditions for the act. It cannot supply the conviction. Same shape as Epistle 009: the smelter needs a telos the corpus alone cannot give. Here the telos is the codebase's, and the holder is human.

**tensions.**

- *Owner of step 4 — genuinely open.* §6.6 demands a frozen corpus per reasoning surface. §3.2 names the ratification conditions. Neither names who owns the compress-and-freeze for an extracted codebase. Candidate: the human anchor who staked the telos. Candidate: a new agent slot between §6.13 and §6.6. Not decidable from corpus as it stands.
- *§3.16 lands twice.* Once at compression (step 4 — is the corpus honest), once at review (step 1 — is the fracture real). Same residual, two moments. Whether one review gate covers both or each needs its own — flag.
- *Step 2 blocks the sequence, step 3 may not need it.* Telos-staking (step 3) is human conviction; positional diagnosis (step 2) is substrate placement. Telos may be stakeable before Pavel exists — you can choose where you're going without yet knowing what substrate debt you've inherited. If so, the blocking is partial: the build cannot start, but the telos can. Whether telos staked without diagnosis is sound or premature — open.
