# Epistle 041 — The Generative Build Procedure // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

1. **G4 — Failure-layer determines adjustment-layer; the dominant collapse is escalating a belt anomaly to the core.** Spec and substrate failures are aim-breaks — the build is pointed wrong — and escalate to architecture, possibly to a telos re-reading. Craft and epistemic failures stay local — the aim holds, only the artifact or its because-chain is degraded — and are fixed at the story or information level. The discipline's weight is the guard against mis-escalation: a local failure that triggers an architecture revision is the protective belt thickening to defend the core against pressure it was never under (§13.13 degenerative shape). The build that re-designs every time a module couples badly is degenerating, not improving.
   *Because:* this is §13.13's degenerative-shape diagnostic applied forward to the build loop, fused with §8.6's partition. That fusion is absent from both sources — §8 partitions failures, §13.13 names belt vs. core, neither wires the four onto the two. The mis-escalation-as-belt-thickening reading gives a builder a concrete in-the-moment test for the degenerative move §13.13 could only diagnose backward at scale. Amended: G4's aim-vs-artifact axis is orthogonal to §8.9's relational-vs-intrinsic routing axis — both hold simultaneously. §8.9 governs which posture handles the work; G4 governs how far up the stack to escalate. A spec failure routes to relational-posture work (§8.9) AND escalates to architecture (G4); an epistemic failure routes to relational-posture work (§8.9) but stays local on escalation (G4) because a broken because-chain degrades the record, not the aim.

2. **G3 — Minimal-stories-as-proof is the stopping condition the build loop lacked.** The story set is complete when a solid argument can be made that it proves the architecture serves the telos. A successful counter-argument is a missing story, named. Replaces "build until it feels done" with a falsification-shaped target: the stopping condition is the proof closes.
   *Because:* the corpus had no stated termination criterion for story-set completeness. §8 partitions failures, §11.6 governs calibration, §13.13 diagnoses programmes — none names when a build is provably-enough. G3 stakes the *what* (does the set prove arch-serves-telos) and correctly fences against §11.6: the how-much stays felt craft; G3 only gives the felt judgment a named object. The counter-argument-reveals-a-gap mechanism is the load-bearing part — it makes the target arguable rather than aesthetic.

## Gravel (dropped)

1. **G2 — "Commit, not freeze."** *Reaching toward:* stripping false permanence from "frozen" so telos and architecture read as deliberate-holds-an-act-can-move rather than metaphysically fixed.
   *Because:* the epistle concedes its own case — "Word-choice correction, not new logic. The architecture already runs on the second." The corpus already carries the exact semantics G2 proposes: §2.2 (the gradient tells you how to behave toward an artifact, not how stable the content is; frozen-as-certain ruled out), §3.5 (frozen is a strong coordinate to reason from, not an inviolable constraint; revision goes through supersession), §13.4 (frozen artifacts operated from rather than proved). "Frozen" in this corpus already means a deliberate hold a deliberate act can move. G2 proposes renaming a distinction the corpus draws three times over. Additionally, the warrant is miscited — G2 cites "rhetoric §3.5" for commit-as-ratification semantics, but rhetoric §3.5 is "Topology was discovered, not invented." Intended targets are rhetoric §3.20 (commitment crossing a boundary, neither party edits after) and §3.26 (version control for what the team is committing to).

2. **"Debt is not a fifth primitive."** *Reaching toward:* a temporal reading of debt — accumulated primitive, a dimension running across the four rather than a fifth alongside them.
   *Because:* restates §8.11 (named debts are compositions of the four primitives) and §8.12 (decompose, then route each fraction) — the epistle says so directly ("The corpus is correct"). The one new element, the temporal-accumulation gloss, leans on §8.12's explicitly-open question ("whether a compound debt's primitives are causally independent or one root with several faces — orthogonal vs. ordered") without engaging it. Restatement plus an unearned lean on a frozen open question.
