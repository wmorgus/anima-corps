Gravel Record — Epistle 012: The Session Chain

## Gold (earned weight)

**1. The session-chain link is the relay unit (§19.1), not the message or the turn.**
Smelted: one cook = one link. The §19.1 relay (intent → cook → result) is the chain's quantum; a cook that fans out to five agents over 15 minutes is one link, not forty.
*Because:* §19.1 staked the relay as the journey's quantum; the corpus had no claim about session-record granularity. This extends §19.1 from the journey's unit to the record's unit — stakes that the navigable record cuts at the journey's joints, not the conversation's.

**2. The chain is not the chat log — epistemic-state-ordered vs. event-ordered.**
Smelted: the log is the conversation (every message, every tool call, in order); the chain is the journey (relay units and their deltas). Neither reconstructs the other cleanly — the log has no notion of which messages closed a cook, the chain has no notion of the tool calls inside one.
*Because:* Novel distinction. Rules out chain-as-rendered-log and log-as-source-of-truth. Most interfaces render the second and call it the first.

**3. The germane context statement is an epistemic-state snapshot, not an event-summary.**
Smelted: at each link, not "what happened" but "what was true here" — what was established, what the live question was, what the session was oriented toward. The event-summary is recoverable from the log; the epistemic-state snapshot is the thing the log throws away. Function: re-entry without re-reading.
*Because:* Load-bearing core of the epistle. Extends §6 (file-as-view) to the temporal/session axis — the context statement is a view over the link, rendering epistemic state for re-entry, not the full event record.

**4. The two-pass harvest is structurally forced by §19.4 generativity — not optional polish.**
Smelted: first pass at cook-completion (fresh, written before the live state decays); second pass refines the prior link against the next stated intent. Germaneness is a relation between a result and the next intent; the next intent does not exist at cook-completion.
*Because:* Derivation, not assertion. Inherits §19.4's asymmetry directly — you cannot know the next question until the last result lands. The second pass is the only pass with access to the relation it records. A single fresh pass over-records; a single retrospective pass under-records (the live state has decayed). Both are needed.

**5. Two passes then freeze — immutability dissolves retroactive mutability.**
Smelted: the second pass refines once, against the next intent, then the link freezes. The named mechanism: a mutable link erases what went out with the gravel — context that looked peripheral at cook-time but was load-bearing. If the link were mutable you would have revised it away and lost the record it was ever there. The immutable chain is the only way to ask that question later.
*Because:* §3.16 shape — a revised context statement is indistinguishable from the original without the original. Applies §4.2 append-only at session-chain scale, with the specific erasure mechanism named. The retroactive-mutability tension dissolves: two passes, then frozen.

**6. The gravel/epistle system is the session chain at a different timescale (§7.1, §7.2).**
Smelted: same structure — what was established (context statement), what was staked (intent), what it opened (generative next-intent, §19.4) — ephemeral at session scale, ratified at corpus scale. The two-pass harvest runs independently in both subsystems: lite pans fresh, the verdict refines retrospectively.
*Because:* Checkable self-resonance per §7.2 — the same asymmetry (fresh-then-accurate) recurs independently in the gravel system and the session chain. The recurrence is structural validation that two is the right count, not analogy.

**7. Harvest reliability inherits §3.16.**
Smelted: a link whose two-pass missed ore is wrong and immutable, structurally indistinguishable from an accurate one without independent reconstruction.
*Because:* Names the capacity residual at session-chain scale — the same constraint §3.16 names for prose rationale, inherited here. Named as a known residual, not a defect.
