# Epistle 013 — The Craft Standard // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**The craft gap is an axis gap, not a content gap.** Most craft forms already live in the frozen tier (application §1.1–1.3); what's missing is a drift-stripped reading-axis over them, not finer-grain content. Two exceptions are genuine new-content claims — stable-dependencies, leak-detection — absent, not mis-indexed.
*Because:* corrects §8.10's "missing micro corpus" framing. §8.10 read the gap as wholly-missing content; the truth is mostly re-indexing plus two additions. The correction is the load-bearing finding.

**The supersession spine is the tell.** The corpora are organized for positional diagnosis — the spine tracks drift across a moving world (application §3 Aufhebung). Craft has no drift axis. A module with high coupling is ill-made whether or not the world moved; the spine has nothing to say about it.
*Because:* names the mechanistic reason the corpora cannot serve craft as-is. Not "they lack content" but "their organizing axis answers the other question." Rules out reasoning craft FROM a drift-indexed corpus.

**Strip-the-referents test — craft is intrinsic predication, not degenerate substrate.** Judgment-standard (epistemic, the assessor's lens) ≠ external referent (ontological, a relatum). Remove all external referents: relational debts (spec/epistemic/substrate) dissolve — no relatum; craft survives — high coupling is still high coupling. That asymmetry is the discriminating test.
*Because:* supplies the operational procedure §8.7 asserts ("intrinsic is the complement") but does not give. Defends the fourth primitive against the standard-is-a-referent collapse.

**Stable-dependencies principle — genuine new content.** Dependencies should point toward stability (low efferent / high afferent coupling), distinct from §1.2 DIP which indexes on abstraction. A concrete module can be stable; an abstract interface volatile.
*Because:* neither afferent/efferent measurement nor direction-toward-stability is in the corpus. Absent, not mis-indexed — must be authored, not re-viewed.

**Rule-out: no drift relaxations.** Craft relaxes only against scope (application §1.1's prototype/throwaway/named-expiration clause — intrinsic, not positional).
*Because:* rules out importing the corpora's drift relaxations into craft judgment. Defends §8.6's intrinsic primitive against positional contamination.

**Rule-out: no metrics-as-verdict.** Coupling counts are signals, not the judgment. If craft reduced to a lint threshold the posture map would be three, not two.
*Because:* defends §8.9's two-posture map against a three-posture reduction. Stakes the consequence by fiat: craft stays a judgment posture, not a measurement instrument.

**Rule-out: no telos content.** The ideal of well-made code is not the telos of this build (Epistle 010, §1.1). Craft is referent-free in both directions — no moved world, no staked telos.
*Because:* rules out smuggling project intent into the craft standard. Closes the referent-free claim symmetrically.
