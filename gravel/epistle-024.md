# Epistle 024 — Anima's telos doc // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**G1 — Direction and warrant are separable axes of the resolution slot itself.** Every dated telos resolution splits: direction is the prayer (what it reaches toward, falsified only by changing the reach — §13.1's undated conviction); warrant is the dated bet that makes the prayer worth praying *now* (§13.9's why-now). The two were entangled in the corpus; 024 pulls them apart and stakes the seam as general, not Anima-specific. *Because:* §13.9 stakes the why-now and §13.1 stakes conviction-came-first, but neither states that direction and warrant are separable axes of the resolution slot. Extends 022's prayer/program seam — 022 is frozen and structurally cannot carry this. Rules out: reading a resolution's dating as moving its reach; reading the prayer/program seam as collapsing under economic contingency.

**G2 — §13.9 fills Anima's resolution slot; the dating tension resolves cleanly.** Anima's not-this is the restricted economy of software (§13.9). The apparent conflict — a prayer is falsified only by changing its reach (022), yet this resolution is dated — resolves: the economic precondition is not what Anima reaches toward, it is what made the reaching newly cheap enough to commit to. *Because:* stakes that a dated resolution does not violate the prayer's undatedness, *because* direction and warrant are pulled apart. Load-bearing instantiation of G1.

**G3 — The impersonator test is the selection criterion for telos-level ruling-outs.** The ruling-out set is minimally sufficient when removing any single element lets a wrong-shaped system satisfy the resolution while failing to be Anima. Each entry blocks a distinct impersonator class. *Because:* the corpus has hundreds of "rules out" line-items; nothing previously named which rise to constitutional weight or why. Novel selection criterion — distinct from rhetoric §1.4's concept-ownership test.

**G4 — The sixth ruling-out (not-erasure-based) differs in kind from the §12 five.** Violating §12/§3 paradigm-exclusions makes Anima a *different kind* of software; violating no-erasure (§3.10/§4.2, Epistle 023) makes Anima *unable to know what it has done*. Two-class structure inside the ruling-out slot: paradigm-boundary exclusions vs. constitutional-floor exclusions (audit-substrate). *Because:* 022's formal-cause inversion did not distinguish these two classes; 024 does, and the distinction is what the sixth exclusion forced.

**G5 — Harm-as-consequence beats history-as-provenance at telos level.** Seniority (§3.6) and recency (§3.7) rank the claim's *history*; harm ranks the claim's *consequences*; a telos doc adjudicates toward consequences because reasoning-from-wrong is the failure it exists to prevent. *Because:* §6.5 stakes harm-bearer as the concept-ownership test; 024 extends it into the value-ordering register and names the asymmetry (consequence vs. provenance) that makes harm beat history specifically at telos level.

**G6 — Delivery-gap and content-gap are distinct and co-dependent.** §6a.11 names the delivery failure (Tiers 1–3 not in the substrate); 024 stakes the *content* those tiers must carry. The two must close together — closing delivery without content (or vice versa) re-opens §3.13 laundering: staked content launders authority from outside the store. *Because:* §6a.11 names the delivery failure and §7.4 demands self-application, but the corpus does not name the co-dependency or the laundering risk that binds the two gaps.

## Tensions (open)

- **Direction/warrant separability is a forward claim.** 022 is frozen; 024 stakes the generalization that direction and warrant are separable for the telos. The corpus has not separately stated it. Flagged as a reach, not ratified — candidate for a future §-revision once 022's schema is extended.
- **Fidelity-vs-user-need (§3, ordering 2).** Fragments point fidelity-first (§2.5, §3.5, §6.12), but §15.18 leaves *exactly this posture* explicitly open — how far fidelity bends, where bending becomes defection. Reach, not ratification. Named in 024 as the most important unresolved value ordering in the system; it governs every human-facing interaction.
- **Runtime precedence hole (§6a.9).** Value ordering says the cycle wins; what a runtime agent does *before* the cycle can run, inside a single invocation, is open. 024 inherits §6a.9's open status and does not close it.
- **Exhaustiveness of the six ruling-outs.** The impersonator test names *why* each entry belongs, not *that* the six are exhaustive. Whether a seventh impersonator class exists is open — same shape as 022's "whether all four slots are mandatory."
- **Founder-enforcer not located.** §3.3a names the asymmetry (the corpus wins against the founder); who convenes against the founder when Anima runs solo (§10.5) is not located. The exclusion is stated; its enforcer is not. Same shape as 022's meta-bet — named, not located.
- **Calliope vs. corpus-file.** Whether the telos doc lives in Calliope as the zeroth-class artifact (022) or stays a corpus file is open. §6a.11's delivery gap intersects 024's content gap; neither is closed.
