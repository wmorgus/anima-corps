# Epistle 018 — Instantiation has no home in the frozen tier // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

- **The gap.** Frozen tier holds invariants; timed instantiations (CVE landscape, framework versions, EOL, regulatory state) have no architectural home. *Because:* names a real category the corpus had no slot for — neither a §-claim nor a tier covered it. Stakes the gap clean.

- **Liquidity-laundering.** "Agent generates from context" in a liquid-tier entry is not a liquid contract — it is frozen at the model's training cutoff, a date nobody chose and nobody can read. The dual of §2.4: §2.4 freezes what it calls immutable and calls the freeze authority; liquidity-laundering freezes what it calls liquid and calls the freeze freshness. Both conflate confidence with liquidity (§2.1). *Because:* closes a live category error sitting unnamed in four ur-software corpora; locates §2.4's twin in the liquid tier — same error family, second member. Smelted to logic.md §2.9.

- **Invariant / instantiation distinction.** Two different things wearing one word. Invariant = what makes a good dependency decision (timeless, belongs frozen). Instantiation = this library at this version is EOL today (expires, homeless). *Because:* the distinction is the lever that dissolves the gap; the four corpora collapsed it into "generate from context" and lost it.

- **§3.9 collision dissolved.** Retrieval-at-decision-time ≠ decay. The corpus entry ("look up CVEs at NVD/KEV") is itself a frozen invariant — the retrieval target doesn't move. What expires is the fetched content, at the source, outside the corpus. §3.9/§3.10 stay intact. *Because:* the earlier residual ("corpus rules out expiry; problem needs expiry") was confused — the problem never needed expiry on the *claim*, only on the *fetched content*, which the corpus never holds. Folded into logic.md §2.10's rules-out.

- **Argus was the symptom; security.md §4 is the architecture demonstrated once.** The right fix is not one more agent per timed domain — it is the §4 retrieval-target pattern generalized across all domains. *Because:* relocates the fix from invention to propagation; the homeless tier was the disease, Argus the symptom.

- **Sequencing — conviction before implementation.** Ratify the architecture-claim in logic.md first; the four-corpus edits follow as downstream consequence, not independent stakes. *Because:* editing the corpora first patches the symptom without staking the architectural decision (cf. §6a.11). Smelted into logic.md §2.10's rules-out (no four-corpus edits before ratification).

- **§6a.10 orthogonal.** Different mechanism (substrate traversal vs. external fetch), different source (Calliope-held ratified stake vs. world-held timed fact). §6a.10 governs how the frozen corpus arrives; the retrieval-target model governs what the corpus points at when the fact cannot be frozen. *Because:* names an adjacency that could read as a collision and isn't — resolving §6a.10 either way changes nothing here.
