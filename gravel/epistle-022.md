# Epistle 022 — Telos as zeroth-class artifact // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**G1 — Zeroth-class, defined by the inverted ratifiability test.** First-class (§1.12) = load-bearing + ratifiable + citable + non-derivative. The telos passes all but ratifiable — fails it on purpose: committed-to, not argued-for from inside the system. Terminal node: every derivation-arrow points at it, none point out. A node nothing points out of is not the head of the ladder; it is the floor it stands on. Zeroth.
*Because:* §1.12 grants "vision/telos artifact" a row in the registers table — G1 stakes the row is in the wrong table. Defines zeroth-class by inverting one existing §1.12 test. Rules out: telos-as-highest-ranked-first-class; treating the vision/telos row as commensurable with other registers on the ratification axis.

**G2 — Telos as prayer: a distinct speech act.** Programs and arguments carry completion conditions — execute/crash, won/falsified. A prayer commits a direction under uncertainty; implementation failure does not falsify it. Only one thing falsifies a prayer: changing what you reach toward.
*Because:* new speech act, no corpus counterpart. The corpus names commit (§3.1/§4.9), measure, attest-survival (§1.14) — not commit-direction-under-uncertainty. Stakes what makes the telos categorically un-votable.

**G3 — Pre-justified, not exempt from justification.** The telos rests on a meta-bet — that agency is worth enacting at all — that also lives outside the system and gets no §-number. The telos is pre-justified: justified at a layer the corpus is downstream of. §13.1 says this from the other side (conviction first); G3 names the container conviction lives in. Holds "outside-the-system AND still-answerable" without collapsing it.
*Because:* extends §13.1, adds the container, rules out reading zeroth-class as a justification hole.

**G4 — Four mandatory slots, warranted by the four causes inverted.** The telos doc's shape resolves into all four Aristotelian causes, each inverted via negativa. Self-resonance is the completeness argument: if the four causes are exhaustive, slots mapping onto all four through inversion inherit the exhaustiveness. Resolution ← final cause inverted (not-this, negate the status quo). Ruling-outs ← formal cause inverted (apophatic: form through exclusion). Value ordering ← efficient cause inverted (not motion but where motion breaks). Derivation bridges ← material cause inverted (no content, only arrows — near-empty by design, emptiness is the inversion).
*Because:* extends §13.6 (which used only final-cause-removal); converts the four-causes parallel from decoration to the completeness warrant for this shape and not another.
