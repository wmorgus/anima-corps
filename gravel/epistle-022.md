# Epistle 022 — What counts as a test for Anima // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**gold-1 — Division of labor: Lakatos governs the programme, Stake governs the case.**
Programme = diachronic — is the architecture generating or absorbing, read across the supersession record at scale. Case = synchronic — did this documented reading hold or break. Different objects, different instruments, neither reduces to the other. The metric (F1's directional pilot) is a fourth thing: governs a bet under measurement.
*Because:* extends §13.13 and §13.2 — the corpus stakes the tribunals but left the Lakatos/Stake division of labor implicit; this stakes it. Clean gold, not candidate — does not inherit the candidate tag from gold-4.

**gold-2 — Claim type determines instrument; the falsifiable surface is typed.**
A retrieval claim (F1) is metric-measurable — null, protocol, rate. A ratification claim (F4) is not the same kind of claim. For claims where a quantitative protocol is underpowered or destroys what it measures (F4/F5), a documented disconfirming case is a *harder* falsifier than an underpowered metric: the metric's null is consistent with both "no effect" and "couldn't see it"; the documented failure with neither. The instrument is selected by the claim, not by availability.
*Because:* extends §14 / §14.1 — reframes F4/F5 thinness as correct architecture, not a sensor gap.

**gold-3 — Missing artifact type: `evaluation_record` + `evaluates` edge.**
A judgment rendered (§11.7 ground control) but not measured has no output artifact. `evaluates` interrogates a case under documented reading; `tests` (§4.8) interrogates a claim under measurement — distinct speech acts, both in the §4.8 typed-edge family. `evaluation_record` extends the §4.9 typed-artifact family.
*Because:* §11.7's reading register currently produces nothing ratifiable; this closes the output-artifact gap and supplies the §13.13(2) diagnostic's missing readable input.

**gold-4 — Case library as a CANDIDATE operationalization of the §13.13 programme tribunal — NOT its closure.**
The accumulated `evaluation_record` corpus is proposed as the instrument §13.13 says it lacks: progressive = named-before-delivered, read off each case's anomaly-named date and world-delivered date.
*Because:* §13.13's open clause ("the architecture does not yet have a measurement protocol… cadence is open") STAYS OPEN. This stakes one candidate, not the resolution. Not yet validated in practice.

**gold-5 — Pre-ingestion as a methodological requirement for structure-tests.**
Both conditions get identical informational substrate; Anima pre-ingests into corpus shape, control gets flat context. Holds content constant so a measured difference is attributable to shape, not to one side knowing more. Not a tip — the condition under which a structure-test could actually fail.
*Because:* names the falsifiability precondition for any structure-vs-content comparison. [→ §4.9, §3.11]

**gold-6 — The 020 seam: `evaluation_record` (render-judgment) is adjacent to and distinct from the severity register (attest-survival).**
A register row may cite an `evaluation_record` as the attempt-artifact; two different speech acts at different surfaces. Name the seam, do not fuse the types.
*Because:* extends §1.14 / Epistle 020 — places the new type relative to the register without collapsing them.

**gold-7 — Case library is substrate for the §15.17 reading-skill residual, NOT its closure.**
Naturalistic generalization — a reader recognizing their situation in a documented case — is the transfer mechanism. Recognition still requires a reader who can tell staked from laundered. §15.17's stake holds intact.
