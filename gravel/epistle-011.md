# Epistle 011 — Relay Depth as Journey Topology // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**Relay as universal unit.** Every ur-user journey is one unit run repeatedly: stated intent → Hermes cooks as deep as needed → result returned. Not a sequence of distinct step-shapes; one structure, universal.
*Because:* extends §6.12 carriage from the front-door act into the per-interaction unit the whole journey is built from. Novel — the corpus names carriage but not carriage-as-the-journey-unit.

**Delegatability reads off cook-depth; human position is fixed.** The human sits at the intent level and does not move. Depth is inside the cook. One axis: deep cook → layers absorb → delegatable; cook-depth zero → residual act irreducibly human.
*Because:* unifies the §6.15 telescoping license, the §6.15 Hephaestus `delegate` exclusion, and §1.11 non-delegatability under one axis. Three scattered claims become three points on a line.

**Cook-depth zero = conviction does not traverse; telos-staking is the floor.** No agent layer absorbs the work because the work is conviction. Hermes facilitates (on behalf of, §6.12; §11.7), does not substitute.
*Because:* extends §6.12 / §11.7 / Epistle 010 into the relay frame — names telos-staking as the floor of the depth axis, not a separate kind.

**Generative chained cycles.** Result returned does not close the journey; it unlocks intents the human could not state before. Journey = recursively chained cycles, extending each time a result lands.
*Because:* the actual center of the author's claim. Absent from the original epistle entirely. Names the dynamic structure of journeys — what a journey *is*, not a fixed list.

**Gap diagnostic.** Every workflow gap = deep-relay (missing layer, closeable by building — §6.14, §16.5) or shallow-relay (human act, no substitute, not closeable — §15.17, §15.15).
*Because:* novel diagnostic that sorts the corpus's own gaps by a single test (what cook-depth, can it be carried).

**Specification asymmetry, structural.** Well-specified deep, underspecified shallow — necessary, not oversight. A relay can be specified iff it can be carried; conviction does not carry.
*Because:* extends §13.6 (apparatus exists to carry the *because* toward the telos); reframes the corpus's open shallow-end gaps (§15.15, §15.17) as necessary, not incomplete.

## Tensions

### Resolved

**T1 — sequential vs fan-out.** Original held an unresolved tension between §6.12 sequential carriage and the fan-out a journey seemed to need.
*Resolved:* pre-cook clarification is a directed graph with cycles allowed (human gathers context, Hermes clarifies, back-and-forth), converging to a clean stated-intent state. The cook is sequential under §6.12. §6.11 licenses the back-and-forth in the clarification phase. Two regimes, separated by the stated-intent boundary. Clean.

**T2 — standing to stake new intents.** Original left open whether the human surfacing intents the corpus had not charted exceeded their standing.
*Resolved:* surfaced intents are feedback to system authors via the evolution loop (§13.10, §3.5, §15.15). Not a gap to patch — the generativity is the expected mechanism. Uncharted relay → evolution loop → charted relay. The "we" stakes what it charts; new intents are how the charted set grows.

### Open (survives)

**Is cook-depth a clean scalar or a vector.** Candidate components — human autonomy at the residual act, agent-layer count, Hermes function — may dissociate. If they do, depth is a vector and "shallow/deep" is a lossy projection. The diagnostic survives either way; the *metric* may not. Carried forward unresolved.

## Smelter's contribution

The topology the original epistle drew was the wrong axis — it had the human moving toward and away from the artifact when the human never moves. The smelting did not tighten the topology; it discarded it and rebuilt on the cook-depth axis.

Resolved both author tensions as **clean-under-corpus** (T1 via the §6.11/§6.12 clarification-vs-cook boundary; T2 via the §13.10/§3.5/§15.15 evolution loop), leaving only the scalar-vs-vector metric question open.
