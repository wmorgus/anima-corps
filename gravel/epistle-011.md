Gravel Record — Epistle 011: The Relay

## Gold (earned weight)

**1. Every ur-user journey is one relay unit, iterated → §19.1.**
Smelted: intent stated → Hermes cooks → result returned, run recursively. Journey is this unit, not a sequence of differently-shaped steps.
*Because:* Extends §6.12 from front-door act to per-interaction unit. Names the journey's atom. Rules out journey-as-step-sequence — novel against the corpus, which had §6.12 as threshold act only.

**2. Relay depth is a property of the cook, not the human's position → §19.2.**
Smelted: human fixed at intent level; what varies is how deep Hermes carries the cook. Human never moves toward or away from the artifact.
*Because:* The frame inversion. Old frame had the human moving; this fixes the human and puts variance inside the cook. Load-bearing for everything downstream — delegatability and specifiability both read off cook-depth, not human position.

**3. Delegatability reads off cook-depth and unifies three corpus mechanisms → §19.3.**
Smelted: §6.15 telescoping license, §6.15 Hephaestus `delegate` exclusion (zero synthesis residual = deepest relay), §1.11 telos-staking non-delegatability — three points on one cook-depth scalar.
*Because:* Unifies three previously-separate mechanisms under one reading. Genuine synthesis, not restatement.
*Open tension carried:* scalar vs. vector. If cook-depth's components (residual-act autonomy, agent-layer count, Hermes function) dissociate, depth is a vector and this unification claim fails. The diagnostic corollary survives either way; the unification does not. Author accepts as open; ratified anyway with the dependency named in the §-claim.

**4. The relay output is generative → §19.4.**
Smelted: a returned result unlocks intents the human could not have stated before. The journey chain extends each time a result lands. Not a fixed map — a chain that grows.
*Because:* The missing center of the original draft, surfaced in smelting. Names the dynamic structure: journey *is* a generative loop. Rules out journey-as-fixed-list.

**5. Pre-cook clarification and the cook are two regimes split by the stated-intent boundary → §19.5.**
Smelted: pre-cook = directed graph, cycles allowed (§6.11). Cook = sequential under §6.12, no telescoping. Boundary = moment intent is determinately stated.
*Because:* Reconciles §6.11 (request-type intake variance) with §6.12 (Hermes dialogic/sequential, not licensed for spatial decomposition). The boundary dissolves the apparent tension — cycles live in clarification only.

**6. Uncharted intents surfaced by relay outputs are feedback, not gaps → §19.6.**
Smelted: a relay result unlocking an uncharted intent routes to the evolution loop (§13.10, §3.5). Surfacing new intents is how the charted set grows (§15.15).
*Because:* Names generativity as the expected mechanism, not a hole to patch. The authority structure for surfaced intents is the author loop. Rules out reading the human gathering a new intent as exceeding standing.

**7. A relay can be specified iff it can be carried → §19.7.**
Smelted: deep relays well-specified (meaning traverses layers); shallow relays resist specification (load-bearing act is conviction, conviction does not traverse). The specification asymmetry is structural — shape of the problem, not stage of incompleteness.
*Because:* Reframes the corpus's open shallow-end gaps (§15.15, §15.17) as necessary, not unfinished. Extends §13.6.
*Tension carried:* possible collision with §15's framing of §15.15 and §15.17 as epistemically live open questions. Does naming a gap structurally necessary close it, or sit alongside the live question? Not resolved at ratification; noted in the §-claim and carried forward.

**Diagnostic corollary (under §19.7).** Every workflow gap is either a deep-relay gap (missing agent layer, closeable by building) or a shallow-relay gap (human act with no substitute, not closeable by building).
*Because:* The two-kinds binary is the diagnostic move and earns weight. The corollary survives the scalar-vs-vector tension. The underlying *pattern* it rests on does not earn fresh weight — see gravel below.
