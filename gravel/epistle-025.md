# Epistle 025 — Four Causes, Four Faults // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**G1 — Cause-frame is the primary exhaustiveness ground; §8.7's §1.12-derivation is confirmed and elevated, not superseded.** The four fault primitives (Epistle 015 / §8.6) are Aristotle's four causes read as failures — identity of structure, not analogy: final→spec, efficient→epistemic, material→substrate, formal→craft. The partition's exhaustiveness is grounded externally on the four-cause account — a 2400-year-old independent argument that a made thing has exactly four causes — not internally on §1.12. Exhaustiveness flows Aristotle→partition→§1.12. §8.7's §1.12-derivation inherits this rather than supplying it. The cause-frame also supplies what §8.15 asserts but does not ground: formal is the sole cause intrinsic to the artifact; final, efficient, material are relations to end, maker, world. §8.15 gives the operational test (strip the referents, craft survives); the cause-frame gives the reason that test holds.
*Because:* converts §8.7's exhaustiveness from an internal architectural fact (enumeration against the §1.12 spine) into an inherited external claim. Either the partition inherits Aristotle's exhaustiveness, or naming a software problem that is no cause's failure breaks both at once — §8.8's falsifiability condition and this one become the same condition.

**G2 — §1.12-tiling is independent confirmation of §1.12, not its inheritance.** The cause-frame tiles onto the §1.12 spine: three relational causes (final/efficient/material = end/maker/world) plus one intrinsic (formal). The clean tiling confirms §1.12's three-relationship enumeration from outside — §1.12 inherits Aristotle's exhaustiveness rather than supplying the partition's.
*Because:* names the direction of confirmation. The three-relationship spine is independently right because Aristotle's three relational causes land on it; the spine does not have to carry the exhaustiveness argument alone.

**G3 — Causal interaction between primitives is predicted, not coincident.** The four causes are not independent — final shapes formal, material constrains form. Faults propagate along the cause-dependencies: spec-fault induces craft-fault (purpose drifts, the form built for the old end is now ill-shaped); substrate-drift erodes epistemic legibility (world moves, the because's referent is gone). The cause-frame predicts the interaction structure that §8.6's coincidence model (compound fault = coincident primitives) cannot produce. Takes a position on the §8.12 / epistle-015-gravel open tension (orthogonal vs. ordered; primitive independence may not hold): primitives interact causally because the causes do.
*Because:* converts compound fault from coincidence-to-itemize into prediction-from-structure. This is the framework's load-bearing predictive claim — what the coincidence model could not generate.
*Open flag:* recoverability of the causal chain. The decomposition lists which primitives are present; it may not recover the chain direction — that this craft fault is downstream of that spec fault. Whether the chain is recoverable post hoc or must be recorded at fault-naming time is open (cf. §3.20 — the because of a structural state is invisible until written). If not recoverable, the decomposition is lossy in exactly the dimension the epistle claims as its advantage.

**G4 — Via negativa fault definition + telos/fault duality.** Fault = a seam come undone = exactly one of four causes broken. Complete artifact = all four causes intact = no fault. The telos doc (positive census of causes, §1.11/§1.12) and the fault decomposition (negative census) are front and back of one structure — build the telos doc right and the fault taxonomy is its developed negative.
*Because:* generalizes §13.6's single-direction move (remove the final cause, the rest lose direction) to all four causes, and names the duality §13.6 did not — the taxonomy is not a list to memorize but "which of the four seams is undone."

**G5 — "Fault" earns a vocabulary corpus entry.** Proposed definition: a seam that has come undone; one of four in any made thing. "Debt" = inherited industry shorthand; "fault" = the formal term. Routes to the vocabulary corpus (epistle-014 governs); land in `corpora/anima/corpus_vocab.md` when that file exists.
*Because:* the cause-frame supplies a single formal term covering all four primitives where "debt" carried industry baggage; the term earns a definitional entry, not a §-claim.
