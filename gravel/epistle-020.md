# Epistle 020 — The severity register // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**1. Severity register as §1.13 application.** A cross-cutting doc answering "what was tried against this §-claim, and what survived?" — attestation, not conviction. Splits from logic.md on §1.13's test. *Because:* the load-bearing leg is rate of change — the register updates on every test run, the fastest cadence in the inventory, and that is the proof it cannot live in a supersession-only file. The split is not topic-driven; it is cadence-driven.

**2. Register ≠ §14 — two speech acts.** §14 answers "which commitments carry a falsifiable bet" — conviction-shaped. The register answers "across all §-claims, what scrutiny ran and what held" — attestation-shaped. §14 is a conviction-shaped subset of the register; the register is the whole attestation surface. *Because:* names a new speech act — attest-survival, distinct from commit (§3.1) and measure (§4.9). Without the distinction §14 gets read as already-the-register and the attestation surface stays invisible.

**3. The laundering it prevents — conviction-tier §4.9 dual.** Without the register, a §-claim that survived severe scrutiny and one never probed sit identically as frozen §-claims. "Show me every frozen §-claim with no severity record" is the conviction-level analog of §4.9's "show me stakes with no empirical tests" — the §14.4 instrumentation gap, one tier up. *Because:* this is the register's central because. §4.9 instruments empirical-survival; the register instruments dialectical-survival. The dual makes the gap queryable instead of asserted.

**4. §13.13's standing read.** §13.13 stakes the progressive/degenerative diagnostic and names the missing surface: reading the supersession record at scale. The register IS that read, standing — records what the belt did under each push. *Because:* extension of §13.13, generalizing §14.2.a's null_result_behavior from one F-claim to the whole corpus. Turns a named-but-unbuilt diagnostic surface into a standing one.

**5. Mayo severity + anti-inflation flag.** The discriminating field is "tested how severely," not "tested." A weak test launders credence as much as no test. *Because:* the anti-inflation flag is the register's own anti-laundering guard — most current attempts (tension probes, shitcorping counterarguments) are not designed as severe tests; the register must not inflate survived-probe into survived-severe. Self-resonant with §3.20: the register that exists to prevent laundering must not launder its own rows.

**6. `tests`-edge as substrate spine.** Every register row is the readable face of a `tests` edge (§4.8) from an attempt-artifact to the §-claim it interrogates. *Because:* grounds the register in existing substrate — not a new primitive, a surface over an existing typed edge. This is what keeps the stake from inventing a substrate it does not need.

**7. Composition-discipline doc as §1.13's merge direction.** logic.md §18 (rendering registers) and rhetoric.md §4 (composition rule + cross-corpus seam) both answer "how must I compose into a corpus?" for the same reader (corpus author) at the same rate of change. §1.13 runs in reverse: one question, two homes → collapse to one file. *Because:* first appearance of §1.13's merge direction — the test is not only file-generating, it is also file-collapsing. Stand-alone: the merge-direction observation holds independent of whether the severity register lands. The full stake (what the doc contains, how it inherits from §18 and rhetoric §4 without disturbing the logos/doxa seam) is epistle-021 territory; this entry names the test applies and confirms the merge.

**8. Scanned-and-rejected as via negativa.** §16 known gaps (the epistle pile IS the build queue — ratification cycle working as designed) and ur-software security footers (drift/maintenance, not structural split). *Because:* the rejection is the contribution — future prospectors don't re-pan these two candidates.
