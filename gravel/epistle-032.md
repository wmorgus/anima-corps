# Epistle 032 — Substrate.md + Process.md: The Material and Efficient Cause Docs // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**The §6.12–§6.19 named-agent inventory is material-cause content misfiled in the formal-cause doc.** logic.md is the conviction corpus — what the system IS and rules out (formal cause). The named agents are an inventory of constituents — what the system is *made of* (material cause). They migrate to `substrate.md`; §6.0–§6.11 (slot-license architecture: harm-distinctness, thin-agent, concept-ownership, telescoping-as-behavior-class) stay in logic.md as the formal cause that licenses the slots. *Because:* names the cut between agent-implementation-inventory (material) and agent-topology-principle (formal) — the cut that makes logic.md heavy and mixed at §6. Novel: no prior §-claim distinguishes the inventory from the architecture that licenses it.

**The lifecycle / loop / semver machinery is efficient-cause content living entirely outside the corpus.** The epistle lifecycle, the two loops, the shitcorping steps, and the VERSION semver convention are conviction-grade efficient-cause content (what produces change in the system) that lived in CLAUDE.md as project configuration — reasoned-*about*-by-the-harness, not reasoned-*from*-by-agents. Migrates to `process.md`. *Because:* the worst of the two misfiles — §6 was in the wrong corpus doc; this was in *no* corpus doc at all. Stakes that config-grade text can be corpus material wearing config clothes, and the corpus is the authority config defers to.

**process.md ≠ §3 — adjacent altitudes, not duplication.** §3 stakes the *conviction* that ratification must happen and why (recorded/contestable/external, the governance Anima requires). process.md describes *how to run it* — the state names, the steps, the triggers, the tag commands. process.md cites §3 (and §2.3, §3.2, §3.3, §3.10) as warrant and does not re-argue it. *Because:* the same telos→logic→process altitude relation that runs the whole corpus; resolves the "why not minus three" worry without collapsing how-vs-why.

**Migration ≠ supersession — a §17-grade meta-clarification.** Moving a frozen claim between files negates nothing and replaces nothing — the claim travels with its §-number, content unchanged, only its home changed. Not §3.10 Aufhebung (which negates a predecessor and elevates a successor). Staked as logic.md §17.4. *Because:* the version convention was built for content change and silent on re-homing; without this distinction a content-preserving migration would be mis-recorded as a ratification or supersession event.

**telos.md derivation bridges gain two entries (additive).** `→ substrate.md` (material register) and `→ process.md` (efficient register) close the two blank coordinates the bridge list named by omission. *Because:* additive completion of an already-open list (it carried a "Planned: → composition.md" line), not a rewrite or supersession of telos.md's frozen content.
