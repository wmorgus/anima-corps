4. **anima-core mis-imports the `server` social role (§1.5); correct shape is library + daemon whose hosts own the HTTP surface.** `server` imported auth, routing, process-lifecycle obligations anima-core never owed in a single-user, two-host world (Bench + anima-viz). The library+daemon split is forced by a frozen agent's cadence import, not chosen for tidiness. *Because:* concrete forcing of the split from §6.18.
