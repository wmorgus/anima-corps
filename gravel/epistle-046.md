# Epistle 046 — The Authoring Layer Was Not Carried // Gravel Record

A gap-record epistle: short, factual, formal. It records a real omission — the aZero
build carried the six constellation agents and their tools but not builder and lite, the
corpus authoring layer, which has been running by hand outside the system. The *fact* of
the gap is sound and worth recording. The audit is against the warrants and the fix, where
a factual record reaches past what the corpus has staked.

## Gold (earned weight)

1. **The gap itself — corpus authoring ran ungated outside the system — is a true, load-bearing record.**
   *Because:* this is the kind of claim a gap-record exists to make, and it is checkable. The
   aZero inheritance manifest (notes/azero-inheritance-manifest.md) enumerates what carries:
   the six agent classes, the tool surfaces, relay/dispatch/probe tools. builder.md and lite.md
   appear nowhere in it. The manifest's own framing — "what carries, what dies, the because for
   each call" — makes the omission visible by its absence. Sessions that wrote and shitcorped
   epistles by hand left no relay record and no substrate read. The gap is real and naming it is
   the epistle's actual job. This earns its place.

## Gravel (dropped)

1. **"Every one of those acts is the kind of work aZero exists to gate (§4, §3.3)."**
   *Reaching toward:* corpus authoring is governed work, so running it by hand is running
   governed work ungated, and the corpus says so.
   *Because:* the cited sections do not say this. §4 is the substrate claim — "one append-only
   artifact store... no side channels," the load-bearing fields, supersession-not-mutation. §3.3
   is the externality requirement — "the validator must be external to the thing validated." Neither
   §4 nor §3.3 frames work as something "aZero gates"; §4 says *where* communication is recorded,
   §3.3 says *who* may validate whom. And "aZero" is not a corpus term at all — it is a build-phase
   name from notes/, not a frozen coordinate. So the sentence charges a build-phase gating concept
   to two sections that describe substrate and externality, neither of which is a gating obligation
   on authoring. The honest warrant is one section over and the epistle does not cite it: **§3.3a**,
   whose cultivation-face clause is exactly on point — "a master who exempts their own outputs from
   the quality check (shitcorping sweep) has the name and not the obligation — same asymmetric
   externality, one register over." §6.12a carries the same clause verbatim ("The master shitcorps
   himself first"). *That* is the corpus saying authoring is governed work that may not run exempt.
   The claim is true; the citation is wrong. Re-presented as "§3.3a — the cultivation face does not
   exempt its own authoring from the cycle," with §4/§3.3 cited only as the substrate-and-externality
   conditions the by-hand work bypassed, this would be clean. As written it overcharges to §4/§3.3
   and to a notes-level term.

2. **"builder and lite are tool surface for the face, not entries beside the constellation."**
   *Reaching toward:* 045 settled that a face is configuration, not an agent; therefore the
   authoring agents are configuration the face loads, not constellation entries.
   *Because:* 045's finding is about *intake* faces — Ari/Hermes carrying a human request at a
   configured intake depth (shallow or cultivating). builder and lite are not intake; they author
   and audit the corpus. To call them "tool surface for the face" is to extend 045's face/agent
   distinction to a new function (authoring) without running the §6.1 test 045 ran for intake —
   same harm, different material → face. The epistle asserts the conclusion (tool surface, not a
   constellation entry) without establishing the harm-identity that licenses it. This is the same
   shape 045's own gravel #1 flagged: a generalization staked as if it were a derivation of the
   prior finding. And the epistle's own **Open contradicts the body**: the body says builder and
   lite *are* tool surface for the face, while the Open admits "what translates into a prompt-*
   artifact, what the dojo face needs to dispatch to them... and how that dispatch wires to the
   relay is not yet specified." If the dispatch surface is unspecified, "tool surface for the face"
   is the conclusion the Open says is not yet reached. Demote to: builder and lite *should* live as
   prompt-* artifacts invocable through the face, by analogy to 045 — a proposed extension, the open
   045 explicitly says it leaves for the authoring agents, not a settled placement.

3. **The "pull not push" assertion in the fix section.**
   *Reaching toward:* "The face loads the authoring persona the same way it loads any persona: a
   pull from the substrate at invocation, not a caller push."
   *Because:* 045 left this as a named, unresolved open. Its load-mechanism Open: "How does Hermes
   acquire a face at invocation... The instinct is to pull from Calliope, not have the caller push
   the face in... The instinct is right; the corpus has not staked it, and §6a.10 is the open it
   belongs to." 046 takes that explicit "the corpus has not staked it" and states the pull as the
   mechanism, in the fix section, as though settled. The lean may well be right — §6a.10 is the
   live pull-vs-push surface and pull is what preserves externality at each invocation — but the
   corpus has not ratified it, and a gap-record asserting it flatly resolves 045's open by fiat. The
   epistle inherits 045's open; it should carry it as an open, not close it in passing. Demote to the
   Open, or state it as the same §6a.10 lean 045 staked — instinct, not settled mechanism.

## Tensions (open)

1. **The irony section stakes nothing and is not load-bearing.** "Epistle 045 staked the face
   mechanism. The corpus amendment that ratifies 045... is the natural first piece of work to run
   through that mechanism... The mechanism's first job is to ratify the mechanism." This describes a
   situation — the 045-ratification amendment sits first on the dogfood queue — and makes no claim
   the corpus must warrant. It is a self-referential observation, not a stake. Remove it and the
   gap-record stands unchanged. In a longer epistle a well-aimed irony can carry a point; here it is
   rhetorical flourish on a factual record, and the record does not need it. Not harmful (it asserts
   no false claim, cites nothing it cannot support) but not earning weight either. For a short
   gap-record, cut it or compress it to one line. Held as tension, not hard gravel, because it stakes
   nothing wrong — it just does not pull.

2. **§6.12b is cited correctly but does little work.** The fix leans on the face/agent distinction
   §6.12a carries (correct), and the trailer lists §6.12b. §6.12b's "two expressions of one telos
   authority, not two agents" is about Ari-as-dojo-face and Ari-as-orchestration-authority being one
   agent in two registers — it does not directly speak to builder/lite being tool surface. Faithful
   citation, but it supports the face-is-not-a-second-agent shape only by analogy, not by reaching the
   authoring case. Minor; the §6.12a face/agent mechanism is the one doing the actual work.

## Verdict

Status: liquid

The gap is genuine and worth the record (Gold #1). But a short factual epistle still has to cite
the corpus it leans on, and 046 misses on the one sentence that carries the weight. "The kind of
work aZero exists to gate (§4, §3.3)" charges a build-phase term and two off-target sections; the
honest warrant is §3.3a's cultivation-face clause (a master who exempts his own outputs has the name
and not the obligation), which the epistle does not cite. The fix section then over-settles twice: it
states builder and lite *are* tool surface for the face while its own Open admits the dispatch surface
is unspecified (the 045 generalization repeating), and it asserts pull-not-push as the load mechanism
while 045 explicitly left that as an open the corpus has not staked. The irony section stakes nothing
and does not pull its weight on a record this short.

Path to freeze: re-cite the gap to §3.3a (with §4/§3.3 named only as the substrate/externality
conditions the by-hand work bypassed); demote "tool surface for the face" to a proposed extension by
analogy to 045 (the open 045 leaves for the authoring agents), not a settled placement; carry
pull-not-push as the §6a.10 open it inherits from 045 rather than asserting it; cut or compress the
irony section to one line. None of these touch the gap-record's actual finding — they bring the
warrants into line with what the corpus has staked. Liquid until then.
