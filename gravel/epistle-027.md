# Epistle 027 — The Minimum Viable Telos // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**G1 — MVT floor. Software is teleological at minimum iff three structural requirements hold together: (a) telos held externally to the executor, (b) telos readable/navigable by the executor, (c) a revision path exists.** §1.11 names the full committed stack; it never isolates the floor. This stakes the via-negativa floor under §1.11 — the least a system needs to cross from "we wrote down our goals" into teleological software.
*Because:* §1.11 names everything the stack requires *when committed to as infrastructure* but does not name the minimum. The floor is the novel cut. Ratified as §1.17.

**G2 — (a) is §3.3 read at the telos layer.** §3.3 requires the validator external to the validated; (a) extends this to the *end* external to the thing pursuing it. Telos in model weights or agent prompt-as-intention fails (a): the executor ratifies its own purpose. Self-attestation at the goal level.
*Because:* §3.3 stakes externality at the validation layer; the telos-layer reading is the extension. Novel application, not duplication — names the goal-level instance of self-attestation §3.3 does not isolate.

**G3 — (b) readability/navigability is a novel telos-layer requirement.** Telos must be structured and navigable, not prose intentions the executor cannot traverse. A goals doc in paragraphs the agent never parses fails (b) even passing (a).
*Because:* the corpus stakes structure for citation (§3.11) and lineage (§4.8), but no readability bar at the telos level existed. MVT names it. Genuinely novel.

**G4 — (c) discriminator is the revision path, not artifact count.** Three requirements, not three files. The telos doc, claim registry, and revision protocol may collapse into one document. What separates "we wrote down our goals" from MVT is structure + revision path, not document boundaries. The team with three pristine artifacts and no way to flag divergence is further from MVT than the team with one flaggable document.
*Because:* rules out the impersonator §1.11 cannot rule out alone — the team with a goals doc that *believes* it has teleological software. The discriminator is (c). Novel cut against the artifact-count proxy.

**G5 — Push-delivered telos fails (a).** Push collapses the end into the executor; pull (or held where the executor reads but does not author) preserves externality. MVT closes at the telos layer what §6a.10 leaves open for context-in-general. (b)'s silence on retrieval primitive does not rescue (a): the requirements are not symmetric — (b) permits push, (a) forbids it; the permissive requirement does not override the forbidding one.
*Because:* §6a.10 leaves pull-vs-push [OPEN] for context-in-general; telos delivery is not context-in-general. The asymmetry argument (permissive does not override forbidding) is the load-bearing move. Closes the open surface at one layer without closing it generally. Added in revision.

## Gravel (dropped)

**Append-only two-entry-class minimum.** Mutable store → flag and the recipe it contradicts cannot coexist → divergence erased not surfaced → (c) fails structurally.
*Because:* §4.2 restated at minimum scale, not extended. Append-only-as-condition-for-meaning is already §4.2; reducing it to two-entry scale adds no new cut. Dropped as ore; kept as one illustrative sentence inside (c) — the store mechanism is rhetoric decoration for the revision-path claim, not separate gold.

**(c) as supersession reduction.** "(c) is §3.10's supersession reduced to minimum surface."
*Because:* the supersession framing is true but does not earn separate weight — the floor claim (G1/G4) already carries the revision-path requirement. Kept embedded in the floor, not staked as a standalone claim.

## Tensions (open)

**§6a.10 stake.** G5 closes pull-vs-push at the telos layer; §6a.10 leaves it [OPEN] for context-in-general. The closure is asymmetric — telos delivery is a special case, not the general one. Whether the general §6a.10 silence should now cite the telos-layer closure as a partial resolution is open. Left live: §6a.10 stays [OPEN]; §1.17(a) names the telos-layer instance that does not generalize.

**T1 — does §3.3-at-telos-layer want its own §-claim?** G2 reads §3.3 at the telos layer and lives inside §1.17(a). Whether the externality-at-the-telos-layer extension is load-bearing enough to stand as a §3.x claim in its own right — not just an application folded into the MVT floor — is open. Staked here as a sub-part; candidate for promotion if downstream reasoning leans on it independently of MVT.
