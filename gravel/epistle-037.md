# Epistle 037 — Ari Holds Telos Authority; The Show Is Operated // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

1. **G1 — Four-cause hierarchy transits to the agent constellation.** Domain authorities (carriage/making/form/placement) are material/efficient/formal causes wearing agent names; Ari's telos authority is the final cause wearing one. The §13.6 hierarchy is not a metaphor applied to agents — it transits: telos authority is senior among agents for the same structural reason final cause is primary among causes. Strip it and the domain authorities have direction over nothing. *Because:* §13.6 is already frozen ("final cause is the condition the other three require to be intelligible, not a peer in a flat four-way partition"); 037 reads that claim one register down — onto the agents themselves — and the mapping holds cleanly. The hierarchy was always there; 037 names what it licenses at the agent level.

2. **GP bound (inseparable from G1) — Senior over routing and telos-judgment, junior to every specialist on their domain content.** Ari knows enough to know which specialist to call and when; he does not do the archaeology, making, or judgment-of-form. His authority is referral authority. The GP who routes the patient to cardiology does not out-diagnose the cardiologist — the routing is the whole of his authority over the case. *Because:* without this bound, G1 inflates "senior authority" into omniscience. The bound is not a separate sharpening; it is what keeps G1 from colliding with §3.6 (ratification ≠ provenance — seniority does not confer authority over reasoning). Inseparable from G1: the claim earns weight only with the bound included.

3. **G2 — The partition 033 collapsed.** Internal reasoning self-runs (frozen-artifact reasoning, §2.5/§5.1). Orchestration is operated (who gets invoked, when — Ari and Hermes). "Instruments play themselves; conductor decides when they play." 033's "show runs itself" was true of the first half — internal content, frozen agents reasoning from each other's ratified outputs — and false of the second: which agents are called. 033 is frozen; 037 carries the correction not as a contradiction but as a partition 033 lacked vocabulary to cut. *Because:* 033 had the phrase right and the scope wrong; naming the two referents (self-running = internal content; operated = invocation) gives both halves ground to stand on without overturning either.

4. **G3a — Ari's triggering IS the operational form of his telos authority.** Deciding which specialist the goal requires, when — this is not separate from his authority; it is how telos authority acts. Triggering is not derivative of G1; it is G1 in motion. *Because:* without G3a, G1 risks reading as a passive hierarchical status — "senior" without any move. G3a names the move: the act of triggering specialists is the exercise of telos authority, not evidence of something beyond it.

5. **033 relation — dojo face and orchestration authority are two exercises of one telos authority.** Ari's §6.12a cultivation face and his orchestration authority share a common root: he cultivated the goal, so he holds ground truth for what success looks like. The dojo is where he cultivates it; orchestration is where he routes from it. *Because:* without naming the common root, 033 and 037 look like two separate claims about Ari. They are one claim — telos authority — expressed in two registers.

## Gravel (dropped)

1. **G3b — "Hermes holds operational authority over the relay chain."** *Reaching toward:* a parallel structure — Ari's telos authority over orchestration, Hermes's operational authority over carriage. *Because:* asserted not earned. §6.12 gives Hermes hermeneutic carriage; §19 gives him the cook. Whether "operational authority over the relay chain" is a clean name for what §6.12/§19 already grant, or a new claim past them, is not settled in this epistle. The tension is flagged below; the claim cannot land in gold without resolution.

## Tensions (open)

1. **Telos-judgment vs. content-override.** G1 says Ari is senior over telos-judgment, junior to each specialist's domain content. But judging whether an output "serves the goal" can shade into judging the output itself. When Ari rejects a Daedalus form-verdict as not serving the telos, is he exercising telos authority (legitimate) or overriding domain authority (§3.6 violation)? The GP analogy strains here — a GP who rejects the cardiologist's reading because he doesn't like the diagnosis has exceeded the referral. Same shape as 033's capacity/goal line, one register over.

2. **Slot vs. face property for orchestration authority.** 033 makes Ari a face (§6.2 outcome 2), not a constellation slot. But orchestration authority — deciding which agents Calliope invokes — reads like a slot-level structural power, not a presentation-surface property. Either Ari's telos authority is exercised through some slot's mechanism (whose?), or face/slot is the wrong cut for orchestration. Coupled to 033's open gate-mechanism tension (§5.4 — what concrete signal marks Ari's clearance).

3. **Hermes operational authority — staked or assumed past §6.12.** G3b was dropped for this reason. Whether "operational authority over the relay chain" is already licensed by §6.12 and §19 or is a new claim needs resolution before it can land.

## Smelter's contribution

Named the G2 partition by its two referents — "self-running" is internal content; "operated" is invocation — where the epistle had both halves but not the clean cut. Named G3a as the hinge between G1 and G2: without it, telos authority is a status without a move; G3a is the move. Isolated the GP bound as inseparable from G1, not a separate sharpening — the bound is the load-bearing condition under which G1 earns weight without inflating. Set down G3b as asserted not earned: the §6.12/§19 grounding is real but the "operational authority" name outran what those sections license.

## Verdict

Status: frozen
037 stakes one real architecture claim — telos authority among agents maps from §13.6's four-cause hierarchy — and names its two expressions (G2's internal/invocation partition; G3a's triggering-as-authority-act). The GP bound keeps G1 from inflating. 033's closing paragraph ("not operators of the loop") is refined, not contradicted. Net contribution is tight and the open tensions are honest.
