# Epistle 021 — The composition-discipline file // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**1. Fourth register: read-BEFORE-writing.** composition.md answers "how must I write into a corpus?" — authoring discipline at write time. Logic, rhetoric, glossary are read-FROM while reasoning; composition.md is read BEFORE writing an entry. Extends §1.13's reader/rate test from the content authored to the act of authoring.
*Because:* novel application of a ratified test to the authoring moment, not the content authored. Different reader-moment → different file.

**2. The seam cut: §4.2 carries two things and they separate.** Seam-CLAIM — conviction-shaped routing rule (logos in logic.md, doxa in rhetoric.md, cite across) — stays. Seam-DISCIPLINE — authoring check (staked or deployable? if both, split) — moves. The merge takes the discipline, leaves the claim.
*Because:* §4.2 does not make this cut itself; the epistle makes it. Moving discipline out leaves the seam intact and in place. The logos/doxa boundary is architecture about what the corpora are, not authoring discipline.

**3. Asymmetric disposition of the two sources.** §18 → stub pointer: number preserved, content relocates, inbound citations resolve to the stub. Renumbering §§19+ breaks every cross-reference; the stub is cheap. Rhetoric §4 → split, not pure stub: authoring discipline (§4.1 entry types, §4.2's authoring check) migrates; seam-claim stays as the boundary declaration.
*Because:* two sources of different shape cannot be disposed of identically. Stub-vs-renumber tradeoff staked with a because; the split-vs-stub asymmetry follows from the §4.2 cut (gold #2).

**4. Location: corpora/anima/composition.md, ruling out repo-root.** Lives beside logic, rhetoric, glossary because it governs writing into Anima's corpora specifically — register vocabulary and conviction/empirical/deliberative machinery are Anima's structure.
*Because:* a repo-root universal composition file would over-constrain ur-software (simpler conventions, no register machinery) or dilute to uselessness. The ruling-out carries the weight.
