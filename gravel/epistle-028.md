# Epistle 028 — Telos-Primacy and the Three Partitions // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**Telos-primacy. Final cause is the condition the other three require, not a peer in a flat four-way partition.** §13.6's three failures — exertion without telos, shape without telos, matter without telos — reread as three statements of one dependency, not three independent failures. Remove telos and material/efficient/formal is mechanism without meaning. "Final" is a nominal accident of Aristotle's ordering; call it primary. *Because:* §13.6 listed the four causes flat. This stakes the hierarchy — the list was not the claim, the dependency is. Rules out: reading §13.6 as a flat four-way partition; treating final cause as a peer.

**Attestation class staked — narrow.** Pure observation artifacts (field_record, empirical_record, execution snapshots — five-Ws flat, no commitment bearer) sit outside the four-cause frame AND outside the §1.12 register taxonomy. Committing attestation (security audits, compliance certs, accessibility reviews — backward-evidence that also stakes a claim from the attesting party) carries a commitment bearer and stays inside §1.12's verification/certification registers. The line is the commitment bearer: absent → outside, present → inside. *Because:* closes §15.22's open tension and the Epistle 027 T2 forward-pointer. The prior draft treated attestation as one uniform class outside the spine — over-broad. The narrow cut is what earns weight.

**Telos-boundary as §1.12 amendment note — not a standalone §-claim.** The hinge already exists (§1.12). "Telos-boundary" is vocabulary naming what the hinge crosses: where conceptual crosses into actual, where commitment becomes checkable. *Because:* clarification, not new structure. Carries as a vocabulary addition to existing §1.12 architecture, not a new claim. §1.13's amendment register, not §13's conviction register.

## Gravel (dropped)

**M/E/F → design/transition/code mapping.** Reaching for: an artifact-level tiling of the four causes onto §1.12's register groups. *Because:* re-indexes §1.12 under Aristotelian labels without changing what registers oblige or what fails when they fail. Decoration — the mapping renames, it does not stake.

**§7.1 self-resonance.** Reaching for: same-shape-at-two-scales as independent evidence the abstraction is at the joints. *Because:* this is an instance of §7.1 (the same shape recurs at every scale), not an extension of it. Decorative restatement of a frozen claim.

**§3.11 as final-cause primacy enforcement.** Reaching for: the citation rule read as the schema-level enforcement of telos-primacy — citations anchor to telos at the limit. *Because:* over-extends §3.11. The citation rule is silent on what citations anchor to; it requires at least one artifact-ID, not anchorage to a final cause. The reading imports a constraint §3.11 does not carry.

## Tensions (open)

**Transition as composite.** A post-mortem is attestation-about-the-past + telos-for-the-future; a PR records the change + commits to the new state. Transition artifacts may be Aufhebung composites, not a clean efficient-cause class. Efficient-cause-as-distinct is the more natural read (Aristotle did not collapse efficient into final + material); the composite reading is the challenge. Carried forward, unresolved.

**§8.6 silence.** §8.6's four diagnostic primitives tile software *problems*; this tiles software *artifacts*. Two partitions over the same territory — diagnostic surface vs. artifact surface. Same partition seen twice or genuinely distinct: unexamined.

**discard_record placement.** §4.9's discard_record — five-Ws attestation (event reconstruction of a discard) or change/transition (an act that produced state change)? Arguably both. The partition's edge case, carried forward.
