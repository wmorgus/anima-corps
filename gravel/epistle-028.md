# Epistle 028 — Four Causes, Four Artifacts. Where final cause is buried. // Gravel Record

## Gold (earned weight)

**Artifact gradient: four causes map onto a doc corpus's artifact classes; the final-cause hole sits in a fixed place.** → §13.15.
Material → code, API docs, changelogs; formal → ADRs, schemas; efficient → the prompt that constitutes the executor; final → telos statement, constraints, decisions log. Lower two causes have mature artifact homes; final cause is absent, implicit, or buried. Systematic gap, not incidental — census any corpus and the hole is in the same place.
*Because:* extends §13.6 from a fault census (what is broken) to a documentation census (what is written down). Same four causes, new surface. The located hole is the contribution, not the artifact list.

**Why-now sharpened to executor-substrate: carrier-collapse, not just cost-collapse.** → §13.16.
LLMs removed not only the cost of writing the final-cause register but the head that held it implicitly. Implicit carriage worked while executors had heads; AI executors have none. The final-cause artifact stops being hygiene and becomes load-bearing.
*Because:* extends §13.9 from economy-level cost-collapse to executor-level carrier-collapse — sharper claim, distinct ruling-out (implicit carriage is not recoverable once executors lack heads).

**Default failure mode: efficient cause slides downward into the nearest documented cause.** → §13.17.
Without final cause documented, the executor cannot idle, so it optimizes the causes that are written — formal or material — and polishes. Output is exertion and shape, plating well.
*Because:* makes §13.6 predictive and concretizes §1.11's process-without-purpose ruling-out — names *which* purposeless process you get and why it looks like work. §13.6's list had no direction; this stakes the determinate downward slide.

**Constraints are final cause expressed apophatically — the corpus's own via-negativa at the documentation layer.** → §13.18.
Constraints are not a fifth artifact class; they are the end stated by its boundary. Gives Epistle 026's chef-deviation tension a measurement surface: adaptation if inside the rules-out, defection if across.
*Because:* applies the corpus's own method (every §-claim carries a Rules out; telos.md is built from ruling-outs) at a new layer, and hands 026's open authority question a surface it can be measured against — even with 026's *when* left unresolved.

**Decisions log's why-not records are the project-scale shitcorpus.** → §13.19.
§3.20's discard-as-act run on project knowledge artifacts. Scope is exact: the why-not records specifically — not the whole log — carry the reasoned-against relation (§6.6). Shape of what a project dropped is evidence of what it is for.
*Because:* §3.20/§6.6 at a new scale (project-documentation, not agent reasoning). Scoping was forced by T3 — see Tensions and Smelter's contribution.

**Produced-by and documents-about are orthogonal relations on one artifact.** → §13.20.
The maker-story (efficient, §13.6/Epistle 025) and the content-carried (final, the decisions log) need not name the same cause. "The because" bears both because the two relations index different objects on the same artifact. Corollary: the actor is not a document; the prompt that constitutes it is (§1.12, §6a.2).
*Because:* resolves the seam with frozen Epistle 025 — made visible and staked, not silently carried. A genuine collision with a frozen claim, named rather than waved off; load-bearing for that reason.
